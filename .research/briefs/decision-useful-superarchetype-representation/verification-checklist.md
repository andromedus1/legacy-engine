## Verdict

**APPROVED.** No load-bearing claim received `partial`, `unsupported`, or `passage-absent`. The
campaign may proceed to the remaining research-pipeline disposition steps without a citation-support

## Audit scope and method

- Fresh-context review of exactly these six briefs: `parent.md`, `estimand-target-population.md`,
  `nontransitive-outcome-models.md`, `sparse-selective-evidence.md`,
  `dynamic-metagame-representation.md`, and `validation-decision-utility.md`.
- Read `.research/reference/decision-useful-superarchetype-representation/INDEX.md` and all 32
  source-direct attestations it names.
- Did not read the pre-existing checklist before replacing it.
- Used the supplied brief-only lint result as the syntactic baseline: 151 resolved/non-broken
  citations, zero broken, zero thin, and no unresolved, collision, intra-program, or
  missing-provenance findings. Numeric/version and comparative advisory flags were reviewed
  semantically below.
- Walked each load-bearing citation to the attestation's `## Key passages`, using the attestation
  summary only to preserve the stated scope and limitations. Repeated citations carrying the same
  claim are grouped by brief and line/section so the checklist remains actionable.
- Attested-source-map inventory entries are not treated as independent claims. Project decisions,
  algebraic derivations, and empirical hypotheses are checked under job (b) for false attribution,
  but they are not misclassified as claims made by the literature.

Verdict meanings: `supported` means the attested passages carry the claim as stated; `partial` means
the passage carries only a narrower claim; `unsupported` means it does not carry the claim; and
`passage-absent` means the asserted supporting passage is not present in the attestation.

## Focused checks requested by the dispatcher

### Limited overlap and target population

`estimand-target-population.md` passes. The brief never represents restriction as a more stable
estimate of the unchanged current-meta quantity. It says weak overlap produces imprecision and
specification sensitivity, identifies the fallback as “among the supported portion,” requires
excluded target mass, and labels “target change” as a campaign inference. That is exactly the scope
carried by `[superrep-estimand-limited-overlap]{9}` and reinforced by
`[superrep-estimand-balancing-weights]{7}`. Verdict: **supported**.

### Informative missingness

`sparse-selective-evidence.md` passes. It uses the source to establish only that informative
missingness exists as a modeling problem and that the cited completion result depends on classic
low-rank and sparse-covariate-effect assumptions. It explicitly denies that the source proves
Legacy is MNAR or that those assumptions hold, and recommends audit/sensitivity rather than an
unearned correction. Verdict: **supported**.

### I-squared and thin families

`sparse-selective-evidence.md` passes. The cited sentence limits the source claim to the conventional
test's study-count dependence, I-squared's definition, and interval uncertainty. The following
thin-family interpretation is labeled `Project inference`, does not attribute a literature cutoff,
and uses low I-squared only as “not falsified,” never as proof of homogeneity. The attestation is
abstract-level but substantively adequate for these narrow claims, and the acquisition gap is
disclosed. Verdict: **supported**.
