# Campaign report — decision-useful superarchetype representation

## Seed

The shipped superarchetype arc exposed a methodological gap: grouping archetypes created useful
navigation, but did not by itself create a meaningful family-level object for comparison or deck
selection. Pooling member records risked changing the target population, hiding member-specific
counters, and turning sparse extrapolation into an apparently grounded family result.

The campaign seed was therefore a first-principles research question: **how can the members of a
superarchetype be combined into a representation that supports honest family comparison and better
playable decisions?** Prior-art scouting supplied candidate ideas, but the campaign's task was to
turn them into a falsifiable project contract rather than select a method by analogy.

## Five-facet decomposition

The research question was decomposed into five coupled facets:

1. **Estimand and target population.** Define whose probability the family result represents,
   separate current and synthetic member mixtures, and make composition sensitivity explicit.
2. **Non-transitive outcome model.** Estimate a reciprocal member-pair probability surface that can
   preserve matchup cycles while testing whether family structure deserves to borrow evidence.
3. **Sparse and selectively observed evidence.** Audit observation, dependence, concentration,
   prior influence, direct-versus-indirect conflict, and the conditions requiring typed refusal.
4. **Dynamic metagame representation.** Version outcome state, target-member weights, and taxonomy
   separately so every as-of result is causal, reproducible, and honest about drift.
5. **Validation and decision utility.** Use nested, future-only evaluation to test predictive safety
   and whether family screening improves the final playable member-archetype action.

The parent synthesis integrates these facets into one ordered Stage-0 and offline-benchmark design.

## Quality report

The final artifacts distinguish literature findings, project decisions, algebraic derivations,
empirical hypotheses, and campaign/project inferences. They retain explicit contradictions rather
than smoothing together incompatible target populations, taxonomies, temporal assumptions, or
validation endpoints.

The methodology contract now includes:

- a playable member-archetype action and explicit deployed, member-only, and same-model-direct
  comparators;
- separate mirror-evidence and structural-diagonal target contracts;
- oriented decisive outcomes, reciprocal reversal, and explicit context disposition;
- versioned weight snapshots, taxonomy state, and contemporaneous versus retrospective replay;
- Stage-0 feasibility before model competition;
- deterministic nested selection of one candidate before every outer holdout;
- identical candidate-selection reruns inside every null and sensitivity pipeline; and
- an exhaustive `NOT_EVALUATED` / `FAIL` / `PASS` launch gate.

These are design-level results. No corpus benchmark was executed and no production model was
selected during this campaign.

## Iteration history

The campaign required four focused revision loops after the initial synthesis:

1. **Methodology loop 1 — decision contract.** Tightened the estimand, playable action, comparator
   ladder, future-only evaluation, and the distinction between prediction quality and decision
   utility.
2. **Methodology loop 2 — evaluability and falsification.** Made Stage 0, context disposition,
   taxonomy scenarios, negative controls, recurrence, sensitivity closure, and exhaustive status
   logic operational rather than aspirational.
3. **Methodology loop 3 — target and selection invariants.** Separated mirror observations from the
   structural `p_aa=0.5` target diagonal and specified deterministic nested candidate selection,
   same-model ablation binding, and null/sensitivity reruns.
4. **Passage-support loop — localized source wording.** Narrowed four claims concerning limited
   overlap, informative missingness, and I-squared to the exact attested passages, while labeling
   the campaign-specific target and thin-family consequences as inference.

The loops revised only the identified contracts and attributions; they did not add sources to mask
an unsupported claim or weaken the production gate.

## Final clean-room evaluator result

**Verdict: APPROVED.** The evaluator read only the six briefs. It classified the work as a valid
offline benchmark design (`offline_benchmark_design: true`) and explicitly denied production
authorization (`production_authorization: false`). It found no high- or critical-severity blocker.

The evaluator returned `PASS` for overall coverage, coherence, playable action, same-model screen
ablation, mirror evidence versus structural target mass, outcome/reversal, weights,
taxonomy/replay, Stage 0, nested candidate selection, null/sensitivity selection reruns, exhaustive
status gating, and epistemic labels. The localized source-wording revisions introduced no
methodology gap.

## Limitations

- Approval verifies the coherence and source support of an offline benchmark design; it does not
  demonstrate empirical benefit on the Legacy corpus.
- The campaign does not establish that composition families are exchangeable, that matchup data
  are missing at random, or that any proposed model will satisfy support and convergence gates.
- Stage 0 must still recover or type missing event, player, round, play/draw, format, and dependency
  context and determine whether the benchmark is adequately powered and computationally feasible.
- Numeric launch thresholds must be preregistered from feasibility work rather than inferred from
  the literature or tuned on the outer holdout.
- `confidence: speculative` remains appropriate until future-only evidence satisfies the complete
  launch conjunction.

## Next step

Design and execute Stage 0, then run the preregistered offline benchmark only if feasibility passes.
The three-level superarchetype page remains blocked until evaluability, predictive safety, decision
value against every required comparator, recurrence, null validity, and every registered
sensitivity scenario all pass. A missing requirement remains `NOT_EVALUATED`, not a soft launch.
