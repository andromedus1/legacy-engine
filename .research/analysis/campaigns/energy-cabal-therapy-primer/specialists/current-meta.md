# Current Legacy field for the Energy Cabal Therapy primer

## Basis and use

The recommended preparation field is the top 24 named archetypes in the project's post-May-18
corpus. That corpus contains 4,847 deck records from 204 events through July 30: 3,566 MTGO and
1,281 MTGmelee paper records. Thus its percentages are **published-deck representation**, not a
claim about unique pilots, match entry share, or one local room. `[ecp-current-corpus]{1}` The
official MTGO index independently confirms dense Legacy publication throughout July, including
Challenges, Leagues, and a Showcase Challenge. `[ecp-mtgo-july-index]{1}`
`[ecp-mtgo-july-index]{2}` `[ecp-mtgo-july-index]{3}` `[ecp-mtgo-july-index]{4}`

The 24 named rows total 78.64% of all corpus records; the 82 `Unknown` records remain outside the
field rather than being guessed into an archetype. {inferred: summed} `[ecp-current-corpus]{2}` For
primer organization, archetypes 1–12 are the mandatory core, 13–20 the next preparation band, and
21–24 still merit full plans because each clears roughly 0.9% in this broad corpus. Those bands are
editorial thresholds, not source-defined tiers.

## Disconfirming analysis

I actively tested the local ordering against a separately fetched rolling aggregation. It does
**not** confirm the exact rank order: MTGDecks places Dimir Tempo first at 9.08%, Izzet Cutter second
at 7.05%, Reanimator third at 5.45%, and Tron tenth at 3.44%; the project corpus puts Tron first,
Show and Tell second, Izzet Delver third, and Dimir Tempo fifth. `[ecp-mtgdecks-july-meta]{2}`
`[ecp-current-corpus]{2}` Therefore the local percentages must not be presented as universal
worldwide metagame share. The defensible claim is narrower: they rank this project's mixed
published-deck corpus, and the external list validates much—but not all—of the opponent set.

The check also disconfirms one-to-one label equivalence. MTGDecks separately names Sneak and Show
and Show and Tell, Izzet Cutter rather than both Izzet Delver and Izzet Midrange, Affinity and
Goblin Welder rather than the broad Blue Artifacts label, and UW Blink/UWx Control rather than the
project's Azorius/Jeskai/White Beanstalk partitions. `[ecp-mtgdecks-july-meta]{2}` Exact percentage
comparisons across those taxonomies are invalid without deck-level remapping.

Finally, neither source establishes matchup plans by prose. The plan descriptions above are
marked as inferences from repeated card configurations, and should be checked by the fair/unfair
matchup specialists against actual representative 75s before exact sideboarding advice is frozen.

No unified percentage or forced label mapping resolves these differences. For primer utility, keep
the 24 local archetypes and mention public aliases at matchup headings.
