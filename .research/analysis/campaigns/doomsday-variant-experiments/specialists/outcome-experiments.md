testing; they are not pilot-independent or current-regime rankings.

### Taxonomy sensitivity

Baseline current counts are A0/B5/C4/D3. Plausible threshold changes produce A0/B6/C4/D2,
A1/B4/C4/D3, or A1/B6/C2/D3. The outcome-bearing members move too: raising the value threshold
leaves no standings-backed C entrant and moves the combined B record to 11-7. `[ddx-outcome-db]{4}`

{inferred: classification conclusion} The robust observation is construction-dimensional—sideboard
module, maindeck value, and denial commitments—not a precise ordinal class performance table.
Any final report should show raw construction features beside the class label and avoid suggesting
that B/C/D cut points were empirically optimized.
