# Magic Comprehensive Rules (June 19, 2026)

## Summary

Wizards' current rules text establishes the London mulligan, legal card-name choices, sacrifice,
and flashback mechanics relevant to this deck. These rules settle several Cabal Therapy and Leyline
opening-hand details independently of strategic recommendations.

## Key passages

- Rule 103.5: players normally draw seven. A mulligan shuffles the hand back, draws seven, then
  bottoms one card for each mulligan taken; after keeping, that player cannot mulligan again.
- Rules 103.6 and 103.6a: after mulligans finish, beginning-with-this-card actions are taken in
  starting-player order; a card that may begin on the battlefield is put there at that time.
- Rule 201.4: when an effect asks for a card name, the player must choose a name in the Oracle card
  reference and cannot choose a token name unless it is also a card name.
- Rule 701.21a: sacrificing moves a permanent its controller controls directly to its owner's
  graveyard. Sacrifice is not destruction.
- Rule 702.34a: flashback permits an instant or sorcery to be cast from the graveyard by paying its
  flashback cost instead of its mana cost; a spell cast this way is exiled instead of going anywhere
  else when it leaves the stack.
- Rule 701.23b: when searching a hidden zone for cards with a stated quality, a player may fail to
  find some or all matching cards. This applies to Surgical Extraction's library search, while the
  graveyard and revealed hand are not hidden in the relevant way.
