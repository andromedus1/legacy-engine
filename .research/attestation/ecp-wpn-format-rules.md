# Wizards Play Network format rules

## Summary

The WPN format page states the generic Constructed deck constraints displayed for its formats.
They establish the legal lower bound and sideboard cap relevant to exact swap plans.

## Key passages

- Constructed decks contain at least sixty cards, with no upper limit if the player can shuffle
  without assistance.
- A sideboard contains up to fifteen cards.
- Main deck and sideboard combined contain no more than four copies of an individual card, except
  basic lands.
