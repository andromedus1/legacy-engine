# Improving Multilevel Regression and Poststratification with Structured Priors

## Summary

Gao, Kennedy, Simpson, Gelman, and others examine multilevel regression and poststratification under
extreme nonrepresentativeness. They emphasize that poststratification is not self-validating: its
quality depends on accurate population cell sizes and on representativeness within cells. Their
structured-prior proposal improves the outcome model by encoding relationships among ordered or
grouped cells, but does not remove those target-population and within-cell assumptions.
