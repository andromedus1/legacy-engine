# Ravnica Remastered release notes — Teferi, Time Raveler

## Summary

The Wizards release notes print Teferi, Time Raveler's card text and clarify its timing restriction.

## Key passages

- Under `Teferi, Time Raveler`, the `{1}{W}{U}` planeswalker says each opponent can cast spells only
  when they could cast a sorcery. Its +1 permits its controller's sorceries to be cast as though
  they had flash; its −3 returns up to one artifact, creature, or enchantment and draws a card.
- The notes state that an effect giving an opponent flash does not overcome Teferi's restriction.
- The −3 can be activated with no target to draw a card. If a chosen target is illegal on
  resolution, the ability does not resolve and its controller does not draw.
