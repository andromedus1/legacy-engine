# Dealing with Limited Overlap in Estimation of Average Treatment Effects

## Summary

Crump, Hotz, Imbens, and Mitnik analyze estimation when comparison groups have weak covariate
overlap. They show that weak overlap can create imprecise, specification-sensitive estimates and
derive a rule for restricting analysis to a subpopulation with better overlap. The remedy improves
precision by changing the population of inference; it does not recover the original target's
quantity where support is absent.
