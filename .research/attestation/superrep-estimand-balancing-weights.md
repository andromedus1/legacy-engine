# Balancing Covariates via Propensity Score Weighting

## Summary

Li, Morgan, and Zaslavsky define a family of weighting estimands indexed by an analyst-selected
target population. Their overlap weights emphasize regions that are well represented in both
comparison groups, are bounded, and optimize asymptotic variance within their class. The paper also
warns that trimming can silently leave a study-specific subpopulation and that a statistically
convenient target can be hard to interpret. Its framework applies to descriptive as well as causal
comparisons, but its optimality claims are conditional on the paper's weighting setup and
assumptions.

## Key passages

> “the choice of target population distribution is neither automatic” — p.3, Introduction

> “The remaining sample may only represent a subpopulation of the original targeted population” — p.2, Introduction

> “the overlap weights are bounded between 0 and 1” — p.3, Introduction
