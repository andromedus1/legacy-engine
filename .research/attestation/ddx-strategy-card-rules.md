# Rules text for strategic package cards

The `cards` table was queried by exact card name for the cards used to describe strategic axes.

## Key passages

1. Personal Tutor searches for a sorcery, reveals it, shuffles, and puts the found card on top of
   the library.
2. Veil of Summer conditionally draws after a blue or black spell and prevents the pilot's spells
   from being countered for the turn while granting blue/black hexproof for the turn. Carpet of
   Flowers conditionally adds mana based on an opponent's Islands.
3. Teferi restricts opponent casting timing and can return an artifact, creature, or enchantment
   while drawing a card. Swords to Plowshares exiles a creature and gives its controller life.
4. Wasteland can sacrifice itself to destroy a target nonbasic land. Murktide Regent has delve,
   flying, and size/graveyard-growth text.
5. Hexing Squelcher cannot be countered, has ward requiring two life, prevents the pilot's spells
   from being countered, and gives other creatures the same ward.
6. Tamiyo attacks to investigate and transforms after the pilot's third draw in a turn. Bilbo can
   cast specified card types from the graveyard when attacking and discounts spells cast from
   outside the hand.
7. Witherbloom Charm can trade a sacrificed permanent for two cards, gain five life, or destroy a
   nonland permanent with mana value two or less.
