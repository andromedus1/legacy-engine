# Lorwyn Eclipsed release notes — Hexing Squelcher

## Summary

The Wizards release notes print Hexing Squelcher's card text and a ruling on uncounterable spells.

## Key passages

- Under `0145_MTGECL_Main: Hexing Squelcher`, Squelcher is a `{1}{R}` 2/2 Goblin Sorcerer. Its own
  spell cannot be countered; it has ward—pay 2 life; spells its controller controls cannot be
  countered; and other creatures its controller controls gain the same ward ability.
- A countering spell or ability may still target an uncounterable spell; on resolution it fails to
  counter that spell but performs any additional effects.
