# Secrets of Strixhaven release notes — Veil and Witherbloom Charm

## Summary

The Wizards release notes print the card text and rulings for Veil of Summer and Witherbloom Charm.

## Key passages

- Under `0060_MTGSOS_MysArch: Veil of Summer`, Veil costs `{G}` and says to draw if an opponent
  cast a blue or black spell that turn; spells its controller controls cannot be countered that
  turn; and its controller and their permanents gain hexproof from blue and black for the turn.
- The Veil notes say it has no effect before resolving and can itself be countered. A countering
  spell or ability may still target a protected spell, but it will fail to counter that spell while
  any other effects still happen.
- Under `0244_MTGSOS_Main: Witherbloom Charm`, the `{B}{G}` instant offers one mode: sacrifice a
  permanent to draw two cards, gain five life, or destroy a target nonland permanent with mana
  value two or less.
