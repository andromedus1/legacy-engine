# Current unfair, mana-engine, and graveyard representative lists

## Source structure

The local DuckDB store joins tournament date, URI, player, result, assigned archetype, board, card
name, and count. The observations below were queried read-only. Except where noted, the selected
configuration is the most recent published list for its assigned archetype from July 15 through
July 30, 2026. Selection establishes a current configuration, not a canonical list or matchup win
rate.

## Key passages

1. **Tron and Show and Tell.** GyomeChef's July 29 Tron 5-0 has four Planar Nexus, four Karn, four
   Kozilek's Command, three The One Ring, four Stock Up, seven Forces, two Grim Monolith, and the
   Mine/Power-Plant/Tower package. Youna07's July 30 Show and Tell 5-0 has four Show and Tell, three
   Omniscience, four Atraxa, one Emrakul, four each of Thoughtseize/Force/Stock Up/Tamiyo, and four
   Ancient Tomb; its sideboard pivots into four Barrowgoyf and three Bowmasters.

2. **Doomsday and TES.** Choutin's July 29 Challenge-winning Doomsday list has four Doomsday, four
   Dark Ritual, four Lotus Petal, one Lion's Eye Diamond, one Oracle, eight cantrips/card-flow
   spells, three Daze, four Force, and three Thoughtseize; its sideboard has Barrowgoyf, Murktide,
   Bowmasters, extra counters, and removal. Carlone90's July 30 TES 5-0 has four each of Burning
   Wish, Gamble, Dark Ritual, LED, Petal, Chrome Mox, Mox Opal, and Fantasticar, plus three each
   Beseech and Echo, one Gaea's Will, and one Tendrils; four Thoughtseize enter from its sideboard.

3. **Blue Artifacts and Mystic Forge.** NathanLipetz's July 29 Blue Artifacts 5-0 uses four each of
   Emry, Force, Petal, Bauble, Opal, Pinnacle Emissary, Thoughtcast, Urza's Bauble, and Saga, backed
   by Kappa Cannoneer and Patchwork Automaton. Rerere's July 29 Mystic Forge 5-0 uses four Chalice,
   four Monolith, four Fleshraker, four Kozilek's Command, four One Ring, four Saga, two Forge,
   three Manifold Key, and artifact mana.

4. **Reanimator and Oops.** Lkbkobe's July 30 Grixis Reanimator 5-0 has twelve reanimation spells,
   twelve large creatures, eight free or one-mana discard spells, four Looting, four Ritual, and
   four Petal. Its sideboard contains four Show and Tell and four Stronghold Gambit, allowing a
   non-graveyard entry pivot. ADK7's July 26 Oops list has eight self-mill creatures, Ritual/Petal
   and eight spirit guides, four Summoner's Pact, four Pact of Negation, Narcomoebas, Dread Return,
   and Oracle; its sideboard has Force of Vigor, Leyline of Sanctity, and Thoughtseize.

5. **Lands and Eldrazi.** JackGreenHat's July 30 Lands 5-0 has four Exploration, four Mox Diamond,
   four Loam, four Crop Rotation, four Saga, four Wasteland, three Depths, three Stage, three
   Boseiju, Maze, Tabernacle, and four maindeck Sphere of Resistance. Jackie Conklin's July 27
   Eldrazi sixth-place list has four each of Tomb, Cavern, Temple, Linebreaker, Endless One,
   Fleshraker, Command, Petal, Thought-Knot, Wasteland, and Wastescape Battlemage; four Chalice are
   in the sideboard rather than the main deck.

6. **Aluren and Golgari Landfall.** Vinilopes's July 29 Aluren list is a hybrid: four Aluren/four
   Acererak alongside four Show and Tell, three Atraxa, two Omniscience, one Emrakul, four Petal,
   four Force, and cantrips/Stock Up. Nagato_Boran's July 30 Golgari Landfall 5-0 has four Hogaak,
   four Supplier, four Moonshadow, four Wight, four Bowmasters, four Thoughtseize, three Reclaimer,
   three Safekeeper, and three Push; Force of Vigor and Endurance are prominent sideboard cards.

7. **Painter.** Uburdub's July 21 Painter 5-0 has four Painter, two Grindstone, four each Engineer,
   Welder, Fable, and Saga, six Blasts, Karn, Cauldron, Bridge, artifact bullets, and three Bolt.
   The redundant artifact recursion means removing one combo permanent does not by itself exhaust
   the engine.

8. **Card-verified Dredge.** The newest row assigned Dredge (Albertosd87, July 29) contains no
   dredger, Narcomoeba, Bridge, or Dread Return; it is a Force/Brainstorm Goryo's Vengeance and
   Reanimate deck. To avoid treating that label as descriptive, the representative Dredge list is
   MahfuzVanGogh's July 26 list, selected by requiring Golgari Grave-Troll. It has four each of
   Grave-Troll, Stinkweed Imp, Narcomoeba, Therapy, Otherworldly Gaze, Careful Study, Brainstorm,
   and Force, plus three each of Bridge, Poxwalkers, and Golgari Thug and two Dread Return. Its
   sideboard has four Leyline, four Unmask, six bounce spells, and Ashen Rider.
