## Summary

Jaccard-index reference. J(A,B) = |A∩B| / |A∪B|, a statistic for gauging similarity and diversity of
sample sets. For binary attributes J = M11/(M01+M10+M11). This is the set-based (copy-count-ignoring)
similarity used by the closest MTG prior art (Lucky Paper) for deck clustering — it answers "which
cards overlap" without regard to how many copies.

## Key passages

- Definition: "J(A,B) = |A∩B|/|A∪B|".
- Purpose: the Jaccard index is "a statistic used for gauging the similarity and diversity of sample
  sets."
- Binary form: "J = M11/(M01 + M10 + M11)" where M11 counts attributes where both vectors have 1.
