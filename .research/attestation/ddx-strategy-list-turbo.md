## Key passages

1. The exact published 60 contains four Doomsday, three Personal Tutor, four Dark Ritual, four
   Force of Will, three Daze, three Thoughtseize, and no Wasteland, Teferi, Bilbo, or maindeck
   Murktide Regent.

   Bowmasters, and Tamiyo, plus two Force of Negation and other interaction; therefore “focused”
   describes the maindeck access posture, not an absence of every post-board creature.
