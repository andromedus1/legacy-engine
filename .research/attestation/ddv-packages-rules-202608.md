# Magic Comprehensive Rules — August 2026

## Summary

The Wizards rules text identifies itself as the authority for competitive game play and states an
effective date of August 7, 2026. The relevant sections define restriction priority, spell/ability
timing, triggered abilities, targets, hexproof, ward, and mana retention.

## Key passages

- Rule 101.2: when one effect permits or directs something and another says it cannot happen, the
  prohibiting effect takes precedence.
- Rules 117.1a–b: a player with priority may cast instants; noninstants normally require the
  player's main phase and an empty stack; activated abilities may be activated any time the player
  has priority.
- Rule 307.5: “only any time they could cast a sorcery” means the player must have priority during
  their own main phase with an empty stack.
- Rules 603.2a and 603.2h: triggered abilities can trigger even when spells or activated abilities
  cannot be used, and beginning-of-phase abilities trigger as that phase begins.
- Rule 106.4: unspent mana empties at the end of each step and phase.
- Rules 702.11b–d: hexproof prevents the protected permanent or player from being targeted by the
  relevant opponent-controlled spells or abilities; hexproof from a quality narrows that rule to
  the stated quality.
- Rule 702.21a: ward is a triggered ability that counters an opponent's targeting spell or ability
  unless the opponent pays the ward cost.
- Rules 113.6g and 113.9: an object's own uncounterability text functions on the stack; activated
  and triggered abilities are not spells, and effects that counter only spells cannot counter them.
