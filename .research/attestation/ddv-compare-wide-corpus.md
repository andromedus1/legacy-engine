# Wide-net Doomsday strategic configurations

## Source structure

The refreshed DuckDB store was opened read-only. Current observations use Doomsday lists dated
2026-08-10 or later. Dated comparison observations use lists from 2026-04-20 onward. Card-level
inclusion counts distinguish main deck from sideboard and count stored list rows and case-normalized
pilot names separately. Results are recorded placements or League records, not matchup measurements.
