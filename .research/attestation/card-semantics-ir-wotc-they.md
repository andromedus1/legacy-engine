# Dominaria Frame, Template, and Rules Changes (Wizards of the Coast, 2018-03-21)

## Summary

Official Wizards announcement of the Dominaria-era (April 2018) templating overhaul. The
load-bearing item for oracle-text parsing: Magic's rules templating replaced the singular
"he or she" / "his or her" construction with singular "they/their/them" across card text,
applied to Oracle text (the announcement shows Duress before/after as the worked example; the
Oracle-wide application was handled through the accompanying Dominaria Update Bulletin). The
consequence for any text-pattern system: possessive scoping phrases in the modern Oracle
corpus use "their" (e.g. "each player returns a creature card from their graveyard"), so
pattern vocabularies built around "his or her"/"your"/"opponent's" alone systematically miss
post-2018 possessive templates. Oracle text is a living, retroactively re-templated corpus —
template families change wholesale at editorial events, which argues for versioning semantic
extraction against a dated card snapshot and re-validating on refresh.

## Key passages

> In its desire to always be correct and consistent, Magic's rules templating has always opted
> for the Chicago Manual of Style's method of referring to a person of unknown gender: "he or
> she." — § Singular They

> Duress (current wording): "Target opponent reveals his or her hand. You choose a noncreature,
> nonland card from it. That player discards that card." Duress (updated Oracle wording):
> "Target opponent reveals their hand. You choose a noncreature, nonland card from it. That
> player discards that card." — § Singular They (before/after example)

> And if this change, in addition to saving words, makes our card text more inclusive for
> people that don't identify as either a "he" or a "she," that's super awesome as well.
> — § Singular They
