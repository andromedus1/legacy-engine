# Matrix Completion with Covariate Information and Informative Missingness

## Summary

Jin, Ma, and Jiang study matrix completion when whether a cell is observed may depend on its
unobserved response. Their method jointly models the response matrix, the missingness mechanism,
and covariate effects under explicit low-rank and sparse-effect assumptions. The work is a warning
against treating an observed sparse matrix as a random sample of all possible cells: recovery claims
depend on a model of why cells are absent as well as a model of their values.
