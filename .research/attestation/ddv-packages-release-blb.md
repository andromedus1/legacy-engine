# Bloomburrow release notes — Swords to Plowshares

## Summary

The Wizards release notes print Swords to Plowshares and clarify its life-gain calculation.

## Key passages

- Under `0054_MTGBLB_SpGuests: Swords to Plowshares`, the `{W}` instant exiles a target creature;
  that creature's controller gains life equal to its power.
- The life gained uses the creature's power as it last existed on the battlefield.
