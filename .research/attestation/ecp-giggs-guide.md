# Lucas Giggs, “Mastering Mardu Energy in Legacy”

Guide by the pilot of a 9-3, second-place finish in the 238-player March 15, 2026 MTGO Legacy
Showcase Challenge. Its maindeck matches the project list except Silent Clearing replaces the
project's fourth Thoughtseize; its sideboard instead has Ending, blasts, and a third Priest.
