# Outlaws of Thunder Junction release notes — Abrupt Decay and Mindbreak Trap

## Summary

The Wizards release notes print Abrupt Decay and Mindbreak Trap and clarify the limits of
uncounterability.

## Key passages

- Under `0034_MTGOTJ_CrimeBns: Abrupt Decay`, the `{B}{G}` instant cannot be countered and destroys
  a target nonland permanent with mana value three or less. The notes state that its
  uncounterability defeats ward's countering action even when the ward cost is unpaid.
- Under `0012_MTGOTJ_CrimeBns: Mindbreak Trap`, the instant exiles any number of target spells. Its
  alternative cost is available after an opponent casts three or more spells that turn.
- The Mindbreak Trap notes explicitly say exile removes a spell from the stack without countering
  it, so it works on spells that cannot be countered.
