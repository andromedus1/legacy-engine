# FTSE Russell equity-methodology glossary

## Summary

FTSE Russell's official methodology glossary distinguishes several mechanisms for maintaining a
changing aggregate representation. Constituent eligibility and weights are reassessed at scheduled
reviews; reconstitution rebuilds the opportunity set; percentile bands use different practical
thresholds for entrants and incumbents to limit unnecessary turnover; and a divisor is adjusted for
additions, deletions, and corporate events so index values remain comparable through composition
changes. Review announcement and effective dates separate advance communication from the actual
state transition.

These are operational index conventions, not statistical evidence that such rules improve matchup
forecasts. They nevertheless provide a concrete analogue for separating membership churn, weight
rebalancing, and continuity of the published series.

## Key passages

> “ensure that the index values remain comparable over time.” — Divisor, PDF p. 7

> “allows previous membership to be considered” — Percentile Banding, PDF p. 10

> “higher threshold for new index constituents than existing members” — Percentile Banding, PDF p. 10

> “eligibility and weights of constituents ... are reassessed.” — Review, PDF p. 11
