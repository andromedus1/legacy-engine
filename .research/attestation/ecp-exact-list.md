# Energy Cabal Therapy deck registration

## Summary

The registered list is a 60-card Mardu creature deck with 22 lands and a 15-card sideboard. The
main deck contains 25 creatures, eight one-mana targeted interaction spells (four Thoughtseize and
four Swords), three Cabal Therapies, and two Goblin Bombardments. The sideboard is entirely graveyard hate,
spell-count constraints, activated-ability constraints, and creature-based entry restrictions.

## Key passages

- Main deck creatures: four each of Ajani, Nacatl Pariah; Amped Raptor; Guide of Souls; and Ocelot
  Pride; three each of Hexing Squelcher, Orcish Bowmasters, and Voice of Victory.
- Main deck interaction and engines: four Swords to Plowshares, four Thoughtseize, three Cabal
  Therapy, and two Goblin Bombardment.
- Mana base: eight fetchlands; four Wasteland; two Karakas; two Plateau; two Scrubland; one each of
  Badlands, Elegant Parlor, Plains, and Shadowy Backstreet.
- Sideboard: four Leyline of the Void, three Deafening Silence, two Clarion Conqueror, two
  Containment Priest, two Null Rod, and two Surgical Extraction.
- The sideboard totals fifteen cards. Every nonland card in the main deck has mana value one or two.
