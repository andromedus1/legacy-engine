**plan:**
* prison-aggro: chalice@1 + hamlet tax their engine while sol lands (tomb/temple/eye) put you 2 turns ahead; tks strips, smasher/sire close
* fleshraker is the engine (oracle): "whenever you cast a colorless spell, create a 0/1 spawn" + "whenever ANOTHER colorless creature you control ENTERS, deals 1 damage to each opponent" — spawn tokens entering count; kozilek's command X=n w/ fleshraker out = n damage + n mana in tokens; two fleshrakers double it
* it that heralds: mv≥7 colorless cost {1} less (sire ONLY in this 60) + other colorless creatures +1/+1 — the anthem is the real text, every drone/spawn punches up
* eye of ugin: colorless eldrazi spells −2 (works on kozilek's command — kindred instant eldrazi); {7}{t} = tutor any colorless creature -> endgame inevitability
* cost stack: sire {7} -> eye −2, it-that −1 -> as low as {4}; temple pays double for eldrazi
* linebreaker is {1}{c}{R} devoid: the {R} ONLY comes from countryside / cavern / petal — temple's {c}{c} can't pay it; combat trigger = target creature you control gains haste +X/+0 (X = your eldrazi count) — the burst kill out of nowhere
* battlemage kickers {g} / {1}{u}: countryside ("any color, spend only on creature spells") pays kickers — maindeck naturalize + unsummon stapled to a 3/1; cavern naming eldrazi = it resolves through counters
* petrified hamlet: on etb choose a LAND name — activated abilities of that name shut off; default name wasteland (protect eye/temple/hamlet), vs lands name thespian's stage (stops the depths copy line); their copies still tap for {c}
* smasher: targeted by their spell -> countered unless they discard; tks: exile the combo piece, remember they draw when he leaves

**mulligans:**
* snap keeps: sol land + chalice + a 2-drop (fleshraker/it-that/battlemage); tomb + temple + tks + threats
* the deck mulls well: 12 sol lands + 4 petal — hands that jump chalice t1 or tks t2 are keeps, fair 4-land hands are not
* g2 vs yard decks: leyline is opener-or-nothing (4-of for exactly this) — weigh a mull toward it vs reanimator/breakfast/dredge
* pitch: no sol-land hands, all-payoff no-mana, chalice-less slow hands vs cantrip decks

**interaction targets — what you save it for:**
* chalice@1: their brainstorm/ponder/swords/bolt/ritual layer — 44% of the field is cantrip decks; NEVER x=0 (counters our petals); our own command at x≥1 is mv≥3, safe
* kozilek's command (choose two, all modes matter):
  * spawn mode + fleshraker = burn to the face + ramp
  * yard mode (exile up to X from graveyards) = MAINDECK gy hate — their murktide fuel, barrowgoyf/nethergoyf food, breakfast/reanimator piles
  * exile creature mv≤X: dead tamiyo/drc/phelia cheap; murktide is mv7 REGARDLESS of delve — x=7 command is 9 mana, 7 with eye out; usually wrong, know it exists
  * scry X + draw = lategame gas
* warping wail (board): counters a SORCERY — show and tell, doomsday, echo of eons; exiles power OR toughness ≤1 — drc, unflipped delver, tamiyo (0/3), ocelot pride
* disruptor flute (board, flash): name = +{3} tax AND activated abilities of that name shut — grindstone (painter dies), shuko (breakfast's equip is {0} activated), lion's eye diamond, aether vial, sneak attack, the one ring, goblin bombardment
* null rod (board): all artifact activated abilities off — their mox opal/keys/LED/vial/grindstone... AND OUR LOTUS PETALS — board petals down with it or accept the nonbo
* leyline of the void: cards going to an OPPONENT'S graveyard exile instead — breakfast's self-mill never lands, reanimate has no target, murktide never delves
* mindbreak trap (board): free if they cast 3+ spells this turn; it EXILES the spells — goes through "can't be countered" (cavern, ad naus protection lines)
* dismember (board): -5/-5; in this deck it is ALWAYS {1} + 4 life (countryside's color mana is creature-spells-only) — plan it with tomb damage; kills emry, guide of souls, phelia, a 6/6 murktide but NOT an 8/8

**board logic recap:**
* 4 flute = the flexible lock — every combo deck has ONE name (grindstone/shuko/sneak/LED/one ring/bombardment); flash means you can name on their draw step read
* 4 leyline = the yard axis (grixis rean 7.6% + breakfast 2.5% + dredge-tail); opener mechanic, all 4 or none
* 2 mindbreak = the stack axis (storm/doomsday/s&t protected turns); exile beats uncounterable
* 2 null rod = the artifact axis (forge/blue artifacts/painter); REMEMBER it shuts your own petals
* 2 dismember = the creature axis (emry/guide/phelia); always {1}+4 life here
* 1 wail = the swiss knife (counter s&t/doomsday, exile drc/tamiyo/pride, surprise blocker)
