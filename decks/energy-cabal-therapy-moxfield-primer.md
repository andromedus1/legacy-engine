# Energy Cabal Therapy — comprehensive Legacy primer

Snapshot: 2026-08-03. This guide is for the exact 75 in
`decks/energy-cabal-therapy-moxfield.txt`. Sideboard plans are recommended starting points derived from
current representative lists; identify the cards you actually see and use the listed branches when
an umbrella archetype differs. Every baseline exchange has equal cards in and out.

## What the deck is trying to do

This is disruptive aggro with a creature-engine core. It is not a prison deck and should not try
to win by drawing hate without pressure. The normal arc is:

1. Start Guide, Ocelot, or discard.
2. Turn every later body into energy, life, Cats, Warriors, or an Ajani transformation.
3. Buy one or two attacks with discard, Swords, Wasteland, Voice, Squelcher, or Bowmasters.
4. Convert temporary or threatened creatures into Therapy flashbacks or Bombardment damage.

Against fair decks, you are usually the go-wide value deck. Against combo, you become disruptive
aggro: interact early, land one relevant lock, and finish before they draw out. Do not keep hands
that contain only one half of that equation.

## Card roles and important interactions

- **Guide of Souls:** the best generic opener. Guide before Ocelot makes Ocelot's entry gain life,
  enabling a Cat that end step. Spend three energy only when flying/counters change the race or
  protect the attacker; banking energy improves later turns.
- **Ocelot Pride:** engine, first striker, and sacrifice supply. Ascend is a reward, not the plan
  you should contort early turns to reach.
- **Ajani:** two bodies and the best bridge to the long game. Another Cat dying transforms him.
  Therapy or Bombardment can sacrifice Ajani's token at the exact useful moment.
- **Amped Raptor:** every main-deck nonland costs two or less, so its two energy can pay for every
  hit—but casting restrictions still matter. Have a legal Swords target when possible. Under
  Deafening Silence, a noncreature hit may be uncastable if you already cast a noncreature spell.
- **Voice of Victory:** makes interaction on your turn impossible and mobilizes two Warriors.
  Attack first, then use the Warriors for Therapy postcombat or Bombardment before their end-step
  sacrifice.
- **Hexing Squelcher:** lead it before the spell that must resolve. It is uncounterable, makes your
  spells uncounterable, and makes targeted interaction cost life.
- **Bowmasters:** hold for cantrips when the trigger matters; deploy it proactively when the clock,
  Army, or sacrifice body matters more.
- **Bombardment:** reach, combat control, protection from removal, Ajani enabler, and a way to exile
  opposing Bridges from Below by putting your own nontoken creature into your graveyard.
- **Wasteland:** treat it as a spell unless the rest of the hand already casts its first two turns.
  Do not strand Guide, Thoughtseize, or a two-drop merely because a target exists.
- **Karakas:** saves front-face Ajani and answers many legendary cheated creatures. It cannot target
  transformed Ajani.

### Sideboard nonbos you must remember

- **Containment Priest** exiles your own Ajani when he transforms. It must already be present to
  stop Show and Tell; putting Priest in from that same Show and Tell is too late.
- **Clarion Conqueror** disables your Guide activation and transformed Ajani's loyalty abilities.
  It does not stop Wasteland, Bombardment, or triggered abilities.
- **Leyline** prevents opposing cards from reaching the graveyard, so Surgical may have no target.
- **Null Rod/Conqueror** stop activated artifact abilities—not Mystic Forge's static casting
  permission, Painter's static text, Thoughtcast, or Fleshraker triggers.
- **Deafening Silence** also limits your Thoughtseize, Therapy, Swords, Bombardment, and Raptor
  noncreature hits. Sequence the one noncreature spell that matters most.

## Sequencing and Cabal Therapy

Against an unknown opponent, lead Guide when the hand curves; against known fast combo, lead
Thoughtseize. Thoughtseize before Therapy converts a guess into exact discard. Voice or Squelcher
should precede the commitment they protect. With Voice, attack before sacrificing a permanent
creature because the temporary Warriors can pay Therapy's flashback cost.

Choose Therapy names from this information ladder:

1. A currently revealed hand: take the decisive remaining card or a duplicated card.
2. Observed actions: fetches, cantrips, passes, and protected cards narrow the range.
3. Archetype plus your intended line: name what beats the next play, not the deck's most famous card.
4. Truly blind: cast only when the miss is affordable; select the common card most capable of
   beating your immediate plan.

Flash back Therapy when the hand is known; the body is temporary, removal-bound, or surplus; the
sacrifice flips Ajani; or the exact card opens a winning turn. Do not sacrifice your only engine or
shorten a lethal attack merely to obtain information.

## Mulligans

An unknown-matchup keep needs all four:

- a colored source and a credible first two turns (Wasteland is not a colored source);
- a turn-one engine or discard spell;
- follow-through such as a second creature, land, Raptor, Ajani, or interaction;
- pressure plus disruption, or enough of one to let the other arrive.

Matchup adjustments:

- **Fast combo:** require turn-one disruption or a strong postboard hate start plus pressure.
- **Tempo/Wasteland:** prefer two functioning sources or fetch access to Plains. Be skeptical of a
  nonbasic plus Wasteland as the whole mana base.
- **Creature decks:** look for a one-drop/Ajani/Raptor plus Swords or Bombardment.
- **Control:** keep layered threats and conversion engines; one threat plus several removal spells
  is weak.
- **On the play:** Thoughtseize and Wasteland improve. **On the draw:** Swords, Bowmasters, and
  stable mana improve; speculative Therapy and Wasteland-as-land worsen.

Examples: keep `Marsh Flats, Arid Mesa, Guide, Ocelot, Ajani, Swords, Raptor` unknown. Keep
`Marsh Flats, Scrubland, Wasteland, Thoughtseize, Therapy, Voice, Raptor` versus combo. Mulligan
`Wasteland, Karakas, Bowmasters, Squelcher, Bombardment, Therapy, Raptor` because it cannot cast its
black/red spells. Mulligan a beautiful Guide/Ocelot/Ajani/Swords fair hand against known fast combo
when it has no relevant interaction.

## Matchups and sideboarding

### 1. Dimir Tempo

**Their plan:** cheap threats backed by discard, Daze/Force, removal, Wasteland, and Kaito; Murktide,
Barrowgoyf, transformed Tamiyo, and postboard Massacre reverse your go-wide edge.

**Counter:** be the go-wide deck. Fetch stable mana, kill Tamiyo before transformation when
practical, save Swords for large blockers, and retain Bombardment/Ajani insurance against Massacre.
Mulligan single-source or discard-only hands. Therapy names: Brainstorm blind; Push to protect one
engine; Force before Bombardment; informed Massacre, Kaito, or the duplicated removal spell.

**Board:** `+2 Clarion Conqueror; -2 Cabal Therapy`. If their list is activation-light and
Barrowgoyf-heavy, make no change, especially on the draw.

### 2. Izzet Delver / Cutter

**Their plan:** Delver/Channeler pressure, Cutter token bursts, Murktide, Bolt, Daze, Force, and
Wasteland. **Counter:** stabilize, Swords Cutter or Murktide, fetch Plains, and resolve Squelcher.
Therapy names: Bolt, Force before Bombardment, or Cutter after a setup cantrip.

**Board:** no change. Only against four Baubles plus additional activated artifacts use
`+2 Null Rod; -2 Cabal Therapy`, preferably on the play.

### 3. Azorius Tempo / Phelia

**Their plan:** Tamiyo, Phelia, Riddler, Stifle/Daze/Force/Wasteland, sometimes Murktide/Teferi.
**Counter:** play around Stifle/Daze, kill Tamiyo, deny profitable Phelia attacks, and lean on
Squelcher/Bowmasters/Bombardment. Keep two colored lands and cheap action. Therapy names: Swords,
Force, Phelia, or a known Wrath.

**Board:** `+2 Containment Priest, +2 Clarion Conqueror; -3 Cabal Therapy, -1 Thoughtseize`.
Priest stops Phelia returns. Against a more controlling build on the draw, retain Thoughtseize and
trim the least useful extra discard/creature based on revealed finishers.

### 4. Azorius Control / UW Blink

**Their plan:** exile removal and counters into Wrath, planeswalkers, Ring/Murktide, or repeated
Phelia/Yorion/Flickerwisp value. **Counter:** pressure in waves, hold the third threat, and prioritize
Bombardment, Voice, Squelcher, and transformed Ajani. Therapy names: Wrath before widening, Force
before Bombardment, Swords/Solitude, or Teferi/Ring.

**Board, control:** `+2 Clarion Conqueror; -2 Swords` (cut two Therapy instead when they show six+
creature threats). **Board, actual blink:** `+2 Priest, +2 Conqueror; -3 Therapy, -1 Thoughtseize`.

### 5. Energy mirror (Boros or Mardu)

**Their plan:** win the same Guide/Ocelot/Ajani/Raptor/Bombardment battlefield; Boros adds
Thalia/Sand Scout/Static Prison, Mardu adds discard and Bowmasters. **Counter:** preserve material,
Swords the engine or profitable Ajani, and win Bombardment exchanges. Therapy names: Swords,
Bombardment, Static Prison, then revealed duplicates.

**Board:** no change. The narrow sideboard is worse than the main 60 here.

### 6. Death & Taxes / BW Yorion

**Their plan:** Vial, mana denial, Thalia, Stoneforge, Solitude, Recruiter/blink, and sometimes
Overlord. **Counter:** fetch Plains, kill equipment/blink engines, use Bombardment in combat. Therapy
names: Vial, Swords/Solitude, Stoneforge, or Skyclave.

**Board:** `+2 Null Rod, +2 Containment Priest; -3 Thoughtseize, -1 Cabal Therapy`. Against Vial-light
mono-white, use only `+2 Rod; -2 Thoughtseize`.

### 7. Cradle Control

**Their plan:** mana creatures/Cradle into Zenith, Natural Order, and tutored value. **Counter:** kill
the first mana creature when it denies an explosive turn, Wasteland Cradle, then clock them.
Therapy names: Green Sun's Zenith; Natural Order after fast mana; otherwise the revealed payoff.

**Board:** `+2 Priest, +2 Conqueror; -3 Therapy, -1 Thoughtseize`.

### 8. Lands

**Their plan:** Exploration/Mox/Loam, Sphere, Depths/Stage, Saga, Maze, Tabernacle, and Boseiju.
**Counter:** Leyline Loam, save Wasteland for Stage/Depths, keep Karakas for Marit Lage, and deploy
only enough bodies to pay Tabernacle. Therapy names: Crop Rotation, Exploration, Sphere, or Loam
when Leyline is absent.

**Board:** `+4 Leyline, +2 Surgical, +2 Null Rod; -4 Swords, -3 Bowmasters, -1 Voice`. Against four
Sphere on the draw, retain the boarded-out Voice and cut one Thoughtseize instead.

### 9. Dimir/mono-black midrange

**Their plan:** discard, efficient threats, removal, and graveyard-derived value. **Counter:** go
wide, preserve engines, Swords the threat that dominates combat, and convert removal through
Bombardment/Ajani. Avoid blind Therapy without a stable list read.

**Board:** no change. If Reanimate/Overlord is central, `+2 Surgical; -2 Therapy`.

### 10. Esper/Jeskai/White Beanstalk midrange

**Their plan:** efficient exile removal and counters into Phelia, planeswalkers, Stock Up/Beanstalk,
or broad sweepers. **Counter:** use the Azorius identification test: Priest only for actual blink,
Conqueror for activated engines, and do not overextend into Wrath. Therapy names: the known sweeper,
Swords, Force, or their principal draw engine.

**Board:** baseline `+2 Conqueror; -2 Therapy`. Add `+2 Priest; -1 Thoughtseize, -1 Swords` only when
Phelia/blink is demonstrated. Against activation-light threat piles, make no change.

### 11. Tron

**Their plan:** Planar Nexus/Tron mana into Command, Ring, Karn, Ugin, with Stock Up and Forces.
**Counter:** Wasteland Nexus or the missing piece, then land Rod/Conqueror while attacking. Therapy
names: Command, Stock Up, or Force before hate.

**Board:** `+2 Null Rod, +2 Conqueror; -4 Swords`. Against Thundertrap-heavy builds on the draw,
cut `-2 Therapy, -2 Swords` instead.

### 12. Show and Tell / Omni-Tell

**Their plan:** cantrips/Stock Up and protection into Show and Tell for Omniscience or a legend.
**Counter:** discard Show and Tell, keep Karakas available, use Silence to break setup-plus-combo,
and have Priest in play before resolution. Priest does not answer Omniscience. Mulligan fair-value
hands. Therapy name: Show and Tell, then Force, Omniscience, or the known pivot.

**Board:** `+3 Deafening Silence, +2 Priest; -4 Swords, -1 Bombardment`. If they reveal four large
creature threats, return `+2 Swords; -1 Therapy, -1 Raptor` for game three while retaining the hate.

### 13. Doomsday

**Their plan:** resolve Doomsday through discard/counters, then traverse a five-card Oracle pile;
postboard they may pivot to Goyf/Murktide/Bowmasters. **Counter:** attack hand, mana, and draw access
while clocking. Silence stops Ritual/cantrip into Doomsday; Rod stops LED/Baubles. Therapy names:
Doomsday blind, Force/Daze before hate, or a known draw effect.

**Board:** `+3 Silence, +2 Rod, +2 Surgical; -4 Swords, -2 Bombardment, -1 Ajani`. Against the full
creature pivot, `+2 Swords; -2 Surgical` for game three.

### 14. Blue Artifacts / Affinity

**Their plan:** cheap artifacts enable Opal, Emry, Thoughtcast, Saga, and large synergy threats.
**Counter:** land Rod, but keep Swords because static/triggered engines remain live. Wasteland Saga;
Therapy Force before Rod or Emry when removal covers the large threat.

**Board:** `+2 Rod, +2 Conqueror; -3 Voice, -1 Therapy`. Against Force-heavy builds on the play, cut
a second Therapy rather than the third Voice.

### 15. Grixis Reanimator

**Their plan:** free discard plus Looting/reanimation; current sideboards can pivot into four Show
and Tell and four Stronghold Gambit. **Counter:** mulligan to Leyline plus pressure, back it with
Surgical/Priest, and retain Swords for the pivot. Therapy names: Unmask to protect hate, Reanimate/
Animate Dead, then Show and Tell/Gambit once observed.

**Board:** `+4 Leyline, +2 Surgical, +2 Priest; -3 Voice, -3 Raptor, -2 Bombardment`. Against a list
confirmed to have no alternative-entry pivot, cut `-4 Swords, -3 Voice, -1 Bombardment` instead.

### 16. TES

**Their plan:** artifact mana/Ritual into Gamble, Beseech, or Wish; Echo/Gaea's Will rebuild and
Tendrils kills. **Counter:** discard tutor/engine, land Silence or Rod, then race. Therapy names:
Gamble/Wish blind, Beseech when bargain mana is ready, or the duplicate revealed tutor.

**Board:** `+3 Silence, +2 Rod, +2 Surgical; -4 Swords, -2 Bombardment, -1 Ajani`.

### 17. Eldrazi

**Their plan:** sol-land threats, Thought-Knot, Fleshraker, Command, and postboard Chalice.
**Counter:** treat it as a creature race; Swords the threat that breaks combat, Wasteland only to
buy a full turn, and win through width/Bombardment. Therapy names: Command, Thought-Knot, or Chalice.

**Board:** no change. Only after seeing several activated artifacts use `+2 Rod; -2 Therapy`.

### 18. Mystic Forge Combo

**Their plan:** fast mana into Ring/Forge/Keys and Fleshraker chains, with Chalice and Saga.
**Counter:** discard into Rod, remove Fleshraker, and remember Rod does not stop casting from Forge's
top card. Therapy names: Ring, Forge, Chalice for a one-drop-heavy hand, or Command.

**Board:** `+2 Rod, +2 Conqueror; -3 Voice, -1 Therapy`. On the draw, use only
`+2 Rod; -2 Therapy` because Conqueror may arrive too late.

### 19. Aluren (pure or Show and Tell hybrid)

**Their plan:** Aluren/Acererak creature-cast loop; current hybrids add Show and Tell, Omni, and
large legends. **Counter:** Silence delays setup-plus-enabler but not the creature loop after Aluren
resolves. Priest stops only the Show and Tell creature branch. Therapy names: Aluren versus pure,
Show and Tell versus hybrid, or Force/Veil before hate.

**Board, hybrid:** `+3 Silence, +2 Priest; -3 Raptor, -2 Bombardment`. **Pure:**
`+3 Silence; -2 Bombardment, -1 Raptor`.

### 20. Golgari Landfall / Hogaak

**Their plan:** Supplier/Hogaak graveyard pressure plus Wight/Reclaimer, discard, removal, and
Safekeeper. **Counter:** Leyline removes the explosive axis but keep Swords for the fair game; kill
Safekeeper before the protected threat. Therapy names: Thoughtseize, Push, or Force of Vigor.

**Board:** `+4 Leyline; -3 Voice, -1 Therapy`. Against demonstrably all-in Hogaak, also
`+2 Surgical; -2 Raptor`.

### 21. Dredge

**Their plan:** dredgers create Narcomoebas/Poxwalkers/Bridge material, Therapy strips hate, and
Dread Return or combat ends the game. **Counter:** mulligan to Leyline, back it with Surgical/Priest,
and keep Bombardment to exile Bridge by sacrificing a nontoken creature. Therapy names: Force to
protect hate, the observed discard outlet, or their bounce spell.

**Board:** `+4 Leyline, +2 Surgical, +2 Priest; -4 Swords, -3 Voice, -1 Ajani`.

### 22. Painter

**Their plan:** Painter/Grindstone with Welder/Engineer recursion, Blasts, Saga, Fable, and Karn.
**Counter:** kill recursion creatures, land Rod, use Conqueror as redundancy, then Surgical a combo
piece after discard/removal. Therapy names: Painter, Pyroblast before hate, Engineer, or the known
combo half.

**Board:** `+2 Rod, +2 Conqueror, +2 Surgical; -3 Voice, -2 Raptor, -1 Therapy`. On the draw against
fast Saga/Tomb, cut both Therapies instead of the second Raptor.

### 23. Oops! All Spells

**Their plan:** ritual into a self-mill creature, create free bodies, Dread Return Oracle, protect
with Pact/Force of Vigor/Thoughtseize. **Counter:** mulligan hardest for Leyline plus pressure or two
independent disruptions. Silence slows ritual chains; Priest stops free creatures and Dread Return.
Therapy names should be informed: Pact before hate, Force of Vigor protecting Leyline, or a revealed
self-miller.

**Board:** `+4 Leyline, +3 Silence, +2 Priest, +2 Surgical; -4 Swords, -3 Voice, -2 Bombardment,
-2 Ajani`.

## Tournament shorthand

- **Fair blue:** develop stable mana, go wide, board lightly, respect sweepers.
- **Creature engines:** keep Swords/Bombardment; board only hate that hits a demonstrated engine.
- **Spell combo:** discard + Silence/Rod + clock; trim Swords and Bombardment.
- **Graveyard combo:** mulligan for Leyline, layer Priest/Surgical, preserve pressure.
- **Artifact combo:** Rod is a speed bump unless you also answer static/triggered threats.
- **When uncertain:** board fewer narrow cards. This deck's main 60 is cohesive; its sideboard is
  powerful precisely because it is narrow.
