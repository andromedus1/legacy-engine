**the plan:**
* t1 one-mana threat that snowballs -> Guide of Souls / Ocelot Pride / Ajani
* t2 Voice of Victory or Amped Raptor, start attacking
* energy is the glue: Guide banks {E} off every creature ETB, Raptor/Static Prison pay it in
* Guide dumps {E}{E}{E} on attack -> two +1/+1 counters + flying counter on an attacker, it becomes an Angel
* Ocelot Pride w/ city's blessing (10+ permanents) copies every token that entered this turn -> the deck's actual "win now" button
* Wasteland + fetches + Sunbaked Canyon feed Sand Scout: any land card hitting your yard from anywhere makes a 1/1 Sand Warrior (once per turn)
* Goblin Bombardment turns that token stream into reach + removal

**what Voice of Victory actually does:**
* "Your opponents can't cast spells during your turn." // hard lock on their instant-speed interaction while you attack
* mobilize 2 -> two tapped-and-attacking 1/1 Warriors, sacrificed at next end step
* -> vs blue decks it is the single most important card in the deck. resolve it, then everything else is safe on your turn.

**mulligans:**
* keep: any 2 lands + a 1-drop threat + a follow-up
* keep: t1 Guide/Ocelot on the play basically always
* ship: 1-landers w/o a fetch, 5+ land hands, hands w/ no 1-2 drop
* ship: all-Static-Prison-and-removal hands w/ no clock // this deck loses long games to real card advantage
* Sand Scout wants an opponent w/ more lands than you (ETB Desert tutor) -> it's a mid-game card, not a keep-defining one

**interaction targets — what you save it for:**
* Swords to Plowshares -> their best blocker or the combo creature. NOT their first 1-drop unless it races you.
* Static Prison -> exiles any nonland permanent until it leaves; costs {E} per upkeep to keep. save for the thing you can't attack through (Marit Lage, a resolved Atraxa, a planeswalker). // running out of energy gives it back
* Karakas -> bounces target legendary creature. their Atraxa / Thalia / Emrakul-adjacent legends, AND rebuys your own Ajani/Thalia in a pinch
* Wasteland -> Gaea's Cradle, Ancient Tomb, manlands, Tron pieces. don't burn it on a fetched dual unless you're cutting them off a color
* Goblin Bombardment -> reach for the last 2-4 dmg, or ping off an X/1 blocker. also sac outlet in response to exile removal
