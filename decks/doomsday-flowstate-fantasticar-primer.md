**the two kills (oracle-grounded):**
* **the fantasticar** ({3} legendary artifact vehicle) is the primary payoff: "whenever you cast your FOURTH noncreature spell each turn, you may sacrifice the fantasticar. if you do, create four 4/4 colorless Construct artifact creature tokens with flying and haste" — 16 power of haste fliers out of nowhere
  * also: "whenever you cast a noncreature spell, you may have the fantasticar become an artifact creature until end of turn" (it has flying) — so it's ALSO a fair 3-power flier you can just cast t1–2 and attack with when the combo is walled off
* **doomsday** ({B}{B}{B} sorcery): "search your library AND graveyard for five cards and exile the rest. put the chosen cards on top in any order. you lose half your life, rounded up" — you build a 5-card pile that lets you chain 4 noncreature spells THIS turn (petals/rituals/cantrips) with the fantasticar already down -> 4th cast triggers the sac -> lethal
* **thassa's oracle** ({U}{U}, 1-of backup): etb "if X [devotion to blue] ≥ cards in library, you win" — the classic empty-pile win when they can't stop a creature but can stop the tokens; jace wielder of mysteries (SB) is the 3rd win angle (draw from empty library = win)
* accelerants: **dark ritual** ({B} -> {B}{B}{B}), **lotus petal** ({0}, sac for any color), **lion's eye diamond** ({0}, "discard your hand, sac: add three mana of any one color, instant speed") — LED after doomsday resolves is free mana with no real cost (your hand is the pile-fodder anyway)
* **flow state** ({1}{U}): "look at top 3, take 1 (take TWO if there's an instant AND a sorcery in your graveyard) rest to bottom" — the camp-defining card; digs to doomsday/protection and turns on fast with your own cantrips in the yard

**why fantasticar over pure oracle:** two payoff types + a proactive body = they need to answer you more than once. BUT note consign to memory counters BOTH the oracle etb AND the fantasticar sac (both are triggered abilities) — fantasticar isn't immune, it's just a second angle and a fair clock

---
**plan:**
* you are a fast, disruptive combo: cantrip + flow state to doomsday, protect the turn with fow/daze/thoughtseize, kill with tokens (or oracle)
* deploy fantasticar early when you can — it beats down for 3 in the air and makes the eventual combo one-card-cheaper to assemble; it also baits removal that would rather be a counter
* thoughtseize/duress FIRST vs blue decks — strip the force of will / consign before you go off; you often win the turn after you clear the road, not the turn you draw the kit
* fair backup: fantasticar + murktide/barrowgoyf (post-board) just wins fair games vs decks that overload on combo hate

**mulligans:**
* keep: doomsday + a black source + an accelerant (ritual/petal) + protection or a cantrip to find it; or a fantasticar-first hand with cantrips + disruption
* the 8 cantrips (4 brainstorm/4 ponder) + 3 flow state + consider make it dig hard — one-piece hands with 2 diggers are keeps
* pitch: no black mana, all-payoff-no-mana, or a combo hand with zero protection into a known blue deck
* g2: don't keep a hand that ONLY combos into their known disruption; value the fair fantasticar/murktide beatdown

**what this deck folds to** // the answer to "what's my leyline-of-the-void": it ISN'T graveyard hate
* **countermagic on the combo turn** — force of will, daze, flusterstorm, mindbreak trap (exiles, beats "can't be countered"); you play through it with your own fow/daze + thoughtseize, but an open counter is the thing that kills you
* **consign to memory** — the surgical hoser: counters the oracle etb AND the fantasticar sac trigger (both triggered abilities) AND fantasticar itself (colorless spell). the one card both this and cephalid fear
* **proactive discard** — thoughtseize/duress stripping doomsday or the payoff before you're ready
* **prison / taxes + a clock** — your worst measured matchup is cradle control (~36%); sphere/thorn/trinisphere tax the ritual->doomsday->pile chain, and you're doing it at HALF LIFE, so any fast clock (delver, burn, their own fetch/shock damage) races you from a deficit. dimir/izzet delver (~42–43%) are the disruptive-tempo drains
* NOT vulnerable to: graveyard hate (library combo), creature removal (no combo creature you must protect except the oracle backup), lifegain (you kill from empty board)

**matchups & sideboard** // shares = post-candelabra field (thin n=80); wr = full-corpus/era lean
**vs blue tempo (izzet ~9% / dimir / delver) — the counter war, your softest column:**
* their daze/pierce/fow tax the combo turn; they race you while you're at half life
* thoughtseize/duress the counter, then go off; fantasticar as a fair clock lets you win the long game if they tap out
* in: 2 force of negation, 1 consign to memory, +grind (2 murktide) // out: some rituals/street wraith, tighten the pile

**vs prison / cradle control / post — grind you out if you stumble:**
* just be faster — combo t2–3 before their lock comes online; don't durdle into spheres
* in: 1 long goodbye, fatal push (their mana creatures/hatebears) // out: excess protection with no targets

**vs other combo (mystic forge / cephalid / tes) — speed + disruption race:**
* you're often faster; thoughtseize their turn or win first; consign counters their oracle/colorless payoff too
* in: force of negation, consign to memory // out: some grind

**vs fair creature/midrange (d&t, energy, dimir midrange):**
* post-board this is where barrowgoyf (deathtouch/lifelink grinder) + murktide turn you into a tempo deck; dauthi voidwalker is a shadow clock + steals their bomb + is incidental gy hate
* in: 4 barrowgoyf, 2 dauthi voidwalker, 2 murktide // out: 2 thassa's oracle-plan redundancy (keep 1), flow states down a bit, street wraith

**named-target notes (oracle-checked):**
* cavern of souls name **merfolk** -> thassa's oracle resolves through counters (it's a Merfolk Wizard); or tap for {C} in the pile
* dauthi voidwalker: "if a card would go to an OPPONENT'S graveyard, exile it with a void counter instead" — shuts reanimator/murktide-fuel AND lets you cast their exiled bomb; shadow = unblockable clock
* barrowgoyf sizes off card TYPES in ALL graveyards (deathtouch + lifelink) — a fine blocker/racer that gains you back the doomsday life
