## The subarchetype split (manual instance of idea-subarchetype-discovery)

The corpus does not label Doomsday subarchetypes (`decks.variant` was NULL for all 1849
Doomsday decks). Mechanical rule applied to the 134 current-regime decks and persisted as
variant labels (re-derivable; the labeler may clear them on re-run):

- **Tempo** = mains Murktide Regent or Tamiyo AND NOT Personal Tutor/The One Ring — **n=47**
  (39 online, 8 paper)
- **Turbo** = the inverse — **n=49**
- Neither/both (n=38) left unlabeled — the rule is deliberately conservative; the "neither"
  bucket is a real residue (Oracle-shell piles without either package), not forced into a camp

**Tier honesty: the tempo camp is EVOLVING (n=47, per the canonical 30-99 band), and paper-only is n=8 (speculative) — there is still
no local-field-specific Doomsday Tempo meta.** These lists are predominantly online-informed;
"local-field tuning" is the field-weighted board, not observed local practice.
