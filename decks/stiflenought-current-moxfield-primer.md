**the combo — three ways to cheat the same trigger:**
* **Phyrexian Dreadnought** {1}, 12/12 trample: "When this creature enters, sacrifice it unless you sacrifice any number of creatures with total power 12 or greater."
* the trigger is what kills it. you have three answers, and they are NOT redundant:
  1. **Stifle** {U} — "Counter target activated or triggered ability." the classic. one-shot, but also live on fetchlands, Wasteland, planeswalkers, storm and cascade triggers.
  2. **Dress Down** {1}{U} flash — "Creatures lose all abilities." the Dreadnought has no ETB to trigger. **it also draws a card**, and it sacrifices itself at the beginning of the end step, so the 12/12 gets its trample back on your turn.
  3. **Doorkeeper Thrull** {1}{W} flash flier — "Artifacts and creatures entering don't cause abilities to trigger." a **permanent, repeatable** answer, so every Dreadnought after it is free.
* -> this is why the deck runs 12 enablers for 4 Dreadnoughts. you are not drawing one combo, you are drawing a threat plus any of twelve.

**the hidden Thrull synergy — this is the part people miss:**
* **Nulldrifter** evoke {2}{U}: "When you *cast* this spell, draw two cards" + "Evoke {2}{U} (…it's sacrificed when it enters.)"
* the cast trigger draws regardless — so at worst it is a {2}{U} draw-two.
* but the evoke sacrifice is an **entering** trigger. with **Doorkeeper Thrull** out (or under **Dress Down**), that sacrifice never triggers -> you keep a **7/7 flying annihilator 1** for {2}{U}.
* // same interaction blanks *their* ETBs: Solitude, Ocelot Pride, Endurance, Archon of Cruelty

**the rest of the threat suite:**
* **Abhorrent Oculus** {2}{U} — additional cost: exile six cards from your graveyard; flier that manifests dread on each opponent's upkeep
* **Tamiyo, Inquisitive Student** {U} — flying 1-drop; investigates on attack; flips when you draw your third card in a turn (Dress Down and Nulldrifter both push that along)

**plan:** land a 12/12 on turn 1-2 behind Force of Will/Daze and kill in two swings, or fall back
on a fair UW tempo deck with fliers, Swords, and card advantage. The deck is a *tempo* deck that
happens to have a broken opener — it does not fold when the combo is answered.

**mulligans:**
* keep: Dreadnought + any enabler + a blue source. that is the hand.
* keep: enabler-light hands with Force of Will + Wasteland + cantrips — the fair plan is real
* keep: Thrull + Nulldrifter hands (that is a 7/7 flier plus a 2-for-1)
* ship: Dreadnought with no enabler and no cantrip. ship no-blue.
* // Doorkeeper Thrull has FLASH — you can hold it up and deploy on their end step, then untap and jam

**interaction targets — what you save it for:**
* **Stifle** -> their fetchland on turn 1 only if you have nothing better; otherwise save it for the actual combo turn or a Wasteland on your Tundra
* **Force of Will** -> the removal spell aimed at a resolved Dreadnought, not their cantrip
* **Swords to Plowshares** -> their fastest clock; you have the biggest creature, so trading down is fine
* **Consign to Memory** (SB) -> "Counter target triggered ability or colorless spell." answers Eldrazi cast triggers, opposing Oracle wins, and colorless spells
* ⚠ **your own Dreadnought dies to Dress Down's "creatures lose all abilities"** only in the sense that it loses trample — the sacrifice trigger is already past. Do not panic-Stifle your own Dress Down.

**what beats you:** a resolved Swords/Prismatic Ending on the 12/12 (you are down a card and a
turn), Karakas is irrelevant here, and — the measured problem — **Dimir Tempo**, which has Daze,
Force, and cheap removal for both halves of your deck.

**sideboard (15) — adoption within the era window:**
* **2 Consign to Memory** (75%) — triggered abilities + colorless spells
* **2 Force of Negation** (70%) — free, exiles noncreature spells
* **1 Hydroblast** (62%) — counters a red spell or destroys a resolved red permanent
* **1 Prismatic Ending** (55%) — clean catch-all removal
* **2 Surgical Extraction** (45%) — graveyard combo
* **2 Clarion Conqueror** (41%) — "Activated abilities of artifacts, creatures, and planeswalkers can't be activated." shuts off Retrofitter Foundry, Urza's Saga construct pumps, Grindstone, Vial, and Cradle-style engines. **Lands are not on that list**, so Wasteland and fetches still work — it is not a mana-denial card. It also does not touch your own Dreadnought, whose sacrifice clause is a *triggered* ability.
* **2 Harbinger of the Seas** (41%) — "Nonbasic lands are Islands." ⚠ **symmetric** — it turns *your* Tundra/Meticulous Archive into Islands too, so you cannot cast Swords under it. You run 1 Plains for exactly this reason.
* **1 Wrath of the Skies** (41%) — {X}{W}{W}, destroys everything with MV ≤ the energy you pay. Note your own Dreadnought is **MV 1** and dies to X=1.
* **1 Grafdigger's Cage** (39%), **1 Containment Priest** (37%) — reanimator/Show and Tell hate

**swap budget:** ~8 — 3 Daze, 1 Abhorrent Oculus, 3 Tamiyo, 1 Nulldrifter. The 4 Dreadnought and
the 12 enablers stay; cutting enablers is how you turn a combo deck into a bad tempo deck.
