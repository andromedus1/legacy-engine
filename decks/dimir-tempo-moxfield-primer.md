# Dimir Tempo — Barrowgoyf Grind

*Legacy · post–Undercity Informer regime · tuned for a wide, combo-and-creature local paper meta*

A disruptive blue-black tempo deck that wins by stripping the opponent's plan
(Thoughtseize, counters, Wasteland), landing a cheap evasive threat, and grinding
the long game with card advantage the opponent can't match. This is the **Barrowgoyf
grind** configuration — the higher-resilience version of Dimir Tempo built for a
format where pure low-curve tempo gets punished by prison, combo, and resilient
creatures.

---

## Why this build (and not the Nethergoyf version)

Dimir Tempo splits into two near-equal camps: the **Nethergoyf + Mishra's Bauble**
low-curve tempo build, and this **Barrowgoyf grind** build (more card advantage, a
recursive threat, more countermagic). Over a long sample they perform about the
same — but in the *current* regime the field has shifted toward decks that punish
raw tempo (prison, big-mana, combo, resilient creatures), and the grind build is the
better-positioned half right now. The trade: you give up a little early-game speed
(Nethergoyf's cheap clock + Bauble's free fuel) for a threat base that wins
attrition wars and doesn't fold to a single sweeper or a clogged board.

**The swap from the tempo build:** −3 Nethergoyf, −2 Mishra's Bauble → +2 Barrowgoyf,
+1 Orcish Bowmasters (to 4), +1 Kaito (to 2), +1 Daze (to 3).

---

## The game plan

1. **Disrupt first.** Thoughtseize and flash Orcish Bowmasters tear up combo and
   card-advantage engines; Force of Will, Daze, and Wasteland tax everything else.
2. **Deploy a resilient threat.** Tamiyo flips into a card engine, Murktide ends
   games fast, Barrowgoyf blocks-and-grinds with deathtouch + lifelink, Kaito is a
   hexproof clock that buries the opponent in cards.
3. **Win the long game.** Flow State, Bowmasters, Kaito, and Barrowgoyf's mill-recur
   give you more gas than fair opponents. You are usually the deck that's still
   drawing live cards on turn 8.

You are **favored vs blue tempo and combo** (Izzet Delver, Show and Tell, Doomsday)
and **the underdog vs resilient permanents / fair creatures** (Death & Taxes, Lands,
Blue Artifacts, Eldrazi) — the sideboard is built to attack exactly that bad column.

## The maindeck

**Threats (10)**
- **4 Tamiyo, Inquisitive Student** — {U} flyer that investigates on attack and flips
  to a planeswalker the turn you draw your third card. Your best one-drop: evasive
  clock + card advantage in one slot.
- **4 Orcish Bowmasters** — flash 1/1 (amass) that pings on ETB and every extra
  opponent draw. Punishes cantrips, brainstorms, wheels, and Bowmasters mirrors;
  doubles as instant-speed interaction.
- **2 Murktide Regent** — delve flyer that ends the game in two swings once your
  graveyard is full of spells.
- **2 Barrowgoyf** — {2}{B} deathtouch + lifelink Lhurgoyf; power = card types in all
  graveyards. Blocks anything, gains life vs aggro/burn, and refills your hand when
  it connects. The grind engine.

**Planeswalker (2)**
- **2 Kaito, Bane of Nightmares** — {2}{U}{B} with ninjutsu; a hexproof 3/4 on your
  turn that surveils and draws while you're dealing damage. Nearly impossible for
  fair decks to answer once it's online.

**Card selection / advantage (11)**
- **4 Brainstorm**, **3 Ponder** — the engine; sculpt draws and bury blanks with fetches.
- **4 Flow State** — {1}{U} dig-3-take-1 that becomes dig-3-take-**2** once you have an
  instant *and* a sorcery in your graveyard. Cheap, repeatable selection that also
  feeds Murktide and Barrowgoyf.

**Disruption / interaction (15)**
- **4 Thoughtseize** — proactive hand disruption; your best card vs combo and control.
- **4 Force of Will**, **3 Daze** — free/cheap counters to protect a clock and stop combo.
- **3 Fatal Push**, **1 Snuff Out** — efficient removal (Snuff is free with a Swamp).
- **1 Brazen Borrower** — flash flyer + bounce; tempo and a clock vs control.

**Manabase (19)**
- **4 Underground Sea**, **1 Undercity Sewers** (UB surveil dual), **1 Island**, **1 Swamp**
- **4 Polluted Delta**, **3 Scalding Tarn**, **1 Bloodstained Mire** (fetches)
- **4 Wasteland** — your other disruption axis; tax greedy manabases.

> *Manabase note:* the 3 Scalding Tarn is slightly off the field's diversified fetch
> split (most lists run a wider mix). It works because all your duals carry the Island
> type, but if you want to dodge a Pithing Needle / keep both colors live, diversify
> 1–2 of them into Misty Rainforest / Flooded Strand.

## Matchup & sideboard guide

*Plans are starting points, not gospel — adjust to what you actually see. "OUT" cards
are listed in rough order of how comfortable the cut is.*

### Favored

**Izzet Delver** — A fair tempo fight you win on card quality and Bowmasters.
- IN: 1 Hydroblast, 2 Flusterstorm
- OUT: 1 Snuff Out, 1 Brazen Borrower, 1 Daze

**Show and Tell** — They're creatureless until they cheat a fatty; your removal is dead.
- IN: 2 Flusterstorm, 1 Force of Negation, 1 Echoing Truth (bounce the cheated bomb)
- OUT: 3 Fatal Push, 1 Snuff Out

**Doomsday** — Disruption + counters race their combo turn. Keep Thoughtseize and Daze.
- IN: 2 Flusterstorm, 1 Force of Negation, 1 Surgical Extraction
- OUT: 3 Fatal Push, 1 Snuff Out

### Roughly even

**Painter** — Null Rod turns off Grindstone entirely; Hydroblast mops up the red.
- IN: 2 Null Rod, 1 Hydroblast, 1 Echoing Truth
- OUT: 3 Daze, 1 Brazen Borrower

**Eldrazi** — Break their colorless mana, then sweep. Daze is weak into Chalice + extra mana.
- IN: 1 Harbinger of the Seas, 2 Toxic Deluge, 1 Echoing Truth
- OUT: 3 Daze, 1 Brazen Borrower

**Jeskai / Esper Control** — Grind with Thoughtseize + threats; counters protect them.
- IN: 2 Flusterstorm, 1 Force of Negation, 1 Echoing Truth
- OUT: 3 Fatal Push, 1 Snuff Out

### Unfavored — sideboard tries to flip these

**Death & Taxes** — Sweepers are everything; Daze is dead into their mana denial.
- IN: 2 Massacre, 2 Toxic Deluge
- OUT: 3 Daze, 1 Brazen Borrower

**Blue Artifacts / Izzet artifacts** — Hurkyl's is a tempo blowout; Null Rod neuters them.
- IN: 2 Hurkyl's Recall, 2 Null Rod, 1 Echoing Truth
- OUT: 3 Daze, 1 Snuff Out, 1 Brazen Borrower

**Lands** — Hardest matchup. Wasteland + Harbinger attack the mana; race before Marit Lage.
- IN: 1 Harbinger of the Seas, 1 Echoing Truth
- OUT: 2 Fatal Push (few targets early)

**Stiflenought** — Bounce or kill the Dreadnought in response to the Stifle trick.
- IN: 1 Echoing Truth, 2 Flusterstorm
- OUT: 3 Daze

## Mulligan & play tips

- **Keep hands with disruption + a way to find threats**, not just threats. A turn-one
  Thoughtseize into Bowmasters backup is a better keep than two fatties and no
  interaction.
- **Sequence cantrips around fetches:** Brainstorm is at its best when you can fetch
  immediately after to shuffle away the cards you put back.
- **Bowmasters is interaction, not just a creature** — hold it up vs cantrip decks and
  blink-of-an-eye their Brainstorm/Ponder for value.
- **Don't over-Daze.** On the draw and against decks with far more mana than you, Daze
  is your weakest card — it's the first thing out in most grindy matchups.
- **Respect your bad column.** Against Death & Taxes, Lands, and artifact prison, you're
  the underdog game one — sideboard hard, mulligan for your sweepers/Hurkyl's, and don't
  durdle.
