Sideboard primer — One sideboard to rule them all

Registered 15:
* 2 Barrowgoyf
* 2 Consign to Memory
* 2 Force of Negation
* 2 Hydroblast
* 2 Sheoldred's Edict
* 1 Harbinger of the Seas
* 1 Massacre
* 1 Nihil Spellbomb
* 1 Null Rod
* 1 Toxic Deluge

Universal rule: 3 Fatal Push is dead against creatureless combo and big-mana — usually the first out. Daze is your weakest interaction on the draw and against decks with far more mana than you. Mishra's Bauble is a free cantrip you can cut whenever you need every card to affect the board.

---
Tron (~9–13%, your #1 online) · C —

Their plan: assemble Urza's Tower/Mine/Power Plant for a huge mana burst, then drop oversized colorless bombs (Karn, Ugin, Wurmcoil, Emrakul) ahead of curve.

IN:
* 2 Consign to Memory
* 1 Harbinger of the Seas
* 1 Null Rod

OUT:
* 3 Fatal Push
* 1 Daze

Push has nothing to kill. Harbinger turns their Tron lands into Islands — no colorless, no assembly. Consign hard-counters their colorless payoffs for free (Karn, Ugin, Emrakul are all colorless spells) and any enter-the-battlefield trigger. Null Rod shuts off Expedition Map and their artifact mana rocks, slowing assembly. (No Damping Sphere now — lean harder on Harbinger to break the mana.)

---
Izzet Delver (~8%) · UR —

Their plan: land a cheap one-drop threat (Dragon's Rage Channeler, Murktide) and ride it behind burn and counters — a tempo clock.

IN:
* 2 Hydroblast

OUT:
* 2 Daze

Hydroblast kills or counters their entire red half (Dragon's Rage Channeler, Bolt, Pyroblast) for one mana. Keep Fatal Push — it has targets here — and cut Daze, your weakest card on the draw in a cheap-spell fight.

---
Show and Tell (~7%) · UR —

Their plan: cheat a game-ending fatty (Emrakul, Griselbrand, Omniscience) into play on turn 1–2 via Show and Tell.

IN:
* 2 Force of Negation
* 1 Consign to Memory
* 1 Sheoldred's Edict

OUT:
* 3 Fatal Push
* 1 Daze

Push is dead. Force of Negation counters the Show and Tell itself for free on their turn — your primary answer. If it resolves, Sheoldred's Edict (instant) makes them sacrifice the lone fatty they cheated in (clean — it's usually their only creature). Consign is situational: it catches an Archon of Cruelty ETB or an Omniscience-cast Emrakul, but whiffs on a vanilla Griselbrand — lean on FoN + Edict + maindeck Force of Will/Daze.

---
Energy (~6%) · WR —

Their plan: flood the board with efficient energy-fueled creatures and beat down fast.

IN:
* 1 Massacre
* 2 Sheoldred's Edict
* 1 Toxic Deluge

OUT:
* 2 Daze
* 2 Mishra's Bauble

A go-wide creature flood — sweepers do the work. Massacre wipes their one-drops (-2/-2, and free if they're on a Plains), Toxic Deluge scales over anything bigger, Sheoldred mops up the last threat. Keep Fatal Push; Barrowgoyf's deathtouch + lifelink also walls their whole team if you draw it.

---
Blue Artifacts / Urza (~5%) · U —

Their plan: Urza's Saga + Moxen/cheap artifacts into giant Construct tokens, backed by Karn and the Thopter–Sword engine.

IN:
* 1 Null Rod
* 2 Force of Negation
* 1 Consign to Memory

OUT:
* 2 Fatal Push
* 2 Mishra's Bauble

Null Rod is the answer — it shuts off their Moxen (their mana abilities included), the Thopter Foundry–Sword engine, and any artifact-activated payoff in one card. Force of Negation stops Karn or a key noncreature spell on their turn; Consign also counters Karn (a colorless spell). The Urza's Saga land itself is Wasteland's job.

---
Lands (~5–14%) · RG —

Their plan: grind with recursive lands and Wasteland lock, then win with the indestructible Marit Lage (Dark Depths + Thespian's Stage).

IN:
* 1 Harbinger of the Seas
* 2 Barrowgoyf
* 1 Sheoldred's Edict
* 1 Consign to Memory

OUT:
* 3 Fatal Push
* 2 Daze

Harbinger of the Seas is the best card here — nonbasic lands become Islands, so Dark Depths can't make Marit Lage at all, Thespian's Stage can't copy it, and Cloudpost/Glimmerpost stop ramping; it even blanks Wasteland and Tabernacle. Barrowgoyf is the clock that actually closes before the Wasteland grind locks you out. As insurance against a resolved Marit Lage, Sheoldred (instant) makes them sacrifice the indestructible 20/20 and Consign counters the Dark Depths creation trigger. Push is dead. (Harbinger turns your own duals into Islands too — worth it here.)

---
Doomsday (~4–5%) · UB —

Their plan: cast Doomsday to set a five-card win pile, then draw into Thassa's Oracle for an empty-library kill.

IN:
* 2 Force of Negation
* 2 Consign to Memory

OUT:
* 3 Fatal Push
* 1 Daze

Force of Negation plus maindeck Thoughtseize attack the combo turn. If Thassa's Oracle resolves, Consign (two copies, plus replicate) counters its win-trigger. Push is dead — race with maindeck threats while you hold up disruption.

---
Midrange & the mirror (Jeskai / Dimir / Azorius, ~10% combined) · WUR / UB / WU —

Their plan: trade one-for-one with removal and card advantage, then grind you out with resilient threats and planeswalkers.

IN:
* 2 Barrowgoyf
* 1 Sheoldred's Edict

OUT:
* 2 Daze
* 1 Mishra's Bauble

Transform into the better grind deck. Barrowgoyf is a deathtouch-lifelink threat that dodges sorcery-speed removal and balloons off the graveyards; Sheoldred edicts their planeswalkers (Tamiyo, Kaito). Daze is dead in a topdeck war — you side IN threats, not answers.

---
Grixis Reanimator (~4%) · UBR —

Their plan: Entomb/discard a huge creature (Archon of Cruelty) and Reanimate it on turn 1–2.

IN:
* 1 Nihil Spellbomb
* 2 Force of Negation
* 1 Consign to Memory

OUT:
* 3 Fatal Push
* 1 Daze

Push can't kill a reanimated Archon. Nihil Spellbomb exiles their graveyard in response to the Reanimate/Animate Dead — the target vanishes and the spell fizzles, and it cantrips for a card. Force of Negation counters the reanimation spell on their turn; Consign blanks the Archon's enter-the-battlefield trigger if one lands.

---
Death & Taxes (~3%) · W —

Their plan: white hatebears + Stoneforge under Wasteland/Rishadan Port mana denial — tax your resources and beat down.

IN:
* 1 Massacre
* 1 Null Rod
* 1 Sheoldred's Edict
* 1 Toxic Deluge

OUT:
* 2 Daze
* 2 Mishra's Bauble

A creature deck that taxes your mana — answer the board, not their spells. Massacre is ideal: they're on Plains and you have a Swamp, so it's free, and -2/-2 sweeps Thalia / Mother of Runes / Stoneforge / most bears at once. Null Rod shuts off Aether Vial and the Stoneforge → Batterskull/Jitte plan (no equip, no Jitte). Toxic and Sheoldred clean up the rest. Cut Daze (Wasteland/Port punish you) and Bauble.

---
Eldrazi / Post (heaviest in paper & the local meta) · C —

Their plan: Ancient Tomb / Eldrazi Temple / Cloudpost ramp into oversized colorless creatures (Thought-Knot Seer, Reality Smasher) ahead of curve.

IN:
* 1 Harbinger of the Seas
* 2 Consign to Memory
* 1 Toxic Deluge

OUT:
* 3 Fatal Push
* 1 Daze

Their threats are MV4–5 colorless, so Push whiffs. Harbinger turns Eldrazi Temple / Ancient Tomb into Islands — choke the ramp. Consign (free, replicate) counters their colorless threats on the stack (Thought-Knot Seer, Reality Smasher, Karn are all colorless spells); Toxic Deluge (pay X=5) sweeps whatever resolves. (No Damping Sphere now — Harbinger + Consign carry the choke.)

Painter (~1–3%) · R —

Their plan: Painter's Servant names a color, then Grindstone mills their whole library — a turn 2–3 combo kill.

IN:
* 2 Hydroblast
* 1 Null Rod
* 1 Sheoldred's Edict

OUT:
* 2 Daze
* 2 Mishra's Bauble

Null Rod stops Grindstone outright — its activated mill ability can't be activated, so the combo simply can't fire. Hydroblast counters/kills their red (Pyroblast, the Blasts) and any red threat; Sheoldred edicts the Painter's Servant, and keep Fatal Push for it too.

---
Storm / TES (~1–2%) · UBR —

Their plan: chain rituals for a big storm count, then Tendrils of Agony to drain you out.

IN:
* 1 Null Rod
* 2 Force of Negation

OUT:
* 3 Fatal Push

Null Rod is the surprise tax-replacement — it shuts off Lotus Petal, Lion's Eye Diamond, and Moxen (their artifact ritual mana can't be activated), badly choking the storm count. Force of Negation counters the Tendrils or a key ritual on their turn for free; lean on maindeck Thoughtseize. (No Damping Sphere now — Null Rod is the mana brake.)

---
Dredge (~1–2%) · UBR —

Their plan: dump and recur the graveyard (Bridge from Below tokens, recursive threats) for free — an engine that ignores damage-based removal.

IN:
* 1 Nihil Spellbomb
* 1 Massacre
* 1 Toxic Deluge
* 1 Sheoldred's Edict

OUT:
* 3 Fatal Push
* 1 Daze

Nihil Spellbomb exiles their graveyard — where Dredge lives — buying a key turn and cantripping; but it's a one-shot reset, so back it with sweepers. Massacre and Toxic Deluge clear the Bridge from Below zombie tokens and recursive bodies; Sheoldred takes the last threat. Push just feeds the recursion. (Harder without permanent graveyard exile — race hard once you've reset them.)

IN:
* 2 Force of Negation

OUT:
* 3 Fatal Push
* 1 Daze

Consign counters the Thassa's Oracle win-trigger; Force of Negation counters Dread Return (the backup); Nihil Spellbomb exiles their self-milled graveyard so Dread Return has nothing to bring back. Strip the Balustrade Spy enabler with maindeck Thoughtseize.

Aluren (~2%) · UBG —

Their plan: resolve Aluren, then free-cast a loop of cheap creatures (Cavern Harpy + a drain creature) to kill you.

IN:
* 2 Force of Negation

OUT:
* 2 Daze

Force of Negation counters Aluren (an enchantment) before the engine starts. Once it resolves the matchup is hard (it free-casts a wall of creatures; Edict only nabs a chump). Strip it with maindeck Thoughtseize, keep Fatal Push for Cavern Harpy / Imperial Recruiter, and race. Daze is too slow — they hold up mana.

Pox Loam (Smallpox, ~1% — the local meta local) · B —

Their plan: resource denial — Smallpox plus Life from the Loam recur Wasteland/Strip Mine to strip your lands every turn, grinding you out under discard behind a lone threat.

IN:
* 2 Barrowgoyf
* 1 Nihil Spellbomb

OUT:
* 2 Daze
* 1 Fatal Push

Out-grind it with threats, not answers. Barrowgoyf is a resilient deathtouch-lifelink body that claws back the life their Smallpox and slow clock cost you. Nihil Spellbomb exiles their graveyard to break a Life from the Loam turn — time it on a dredge and they lose the recursion engine (one-shot, so pick your moment). Daze is dead (they aren't racing) and Push is light on targets; lean on maindeck Thoughtseize to strip Loam or their key threat.

---
---
Everything else (Elves, Goblins, Mystic Forge, Cephalid Breakfast, Burn, and rogue brews): apply the universal rule — Push stays vs creatures and comes out vs combo/big-mana; Daze comes out on the draw; Bauble comes out when you need live cards. Creature decks want Massacre / Toxic / Sheoldred; combo wants Force of Negation / Consign / Nihil Spellbomb; ramp & big-mana want Harbinger; artifact decks want Null Rod; grindy fair decks want the Barrowgoyf transform. When in doubt, side toward proactivity — this deck wins by ending the game, not by holding every answer.
