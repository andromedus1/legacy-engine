# Lands — Sphere Prison (Ancient Tomb build)

The prison-leaning build of Legacy Lands: Turn-1 Sphere of Resistance off Ancient Tomb or Mox
Diamond, then strangle spell-based decks while Urza's Saga and Dark Depths + Thespian's Stage win
the game. Every land is a spell here — Life from the Loam rebuys them, Wasteland taxes their mana,
The Tabernacle at Pendrell Vale wipes boards, and Depths ends the game.

This shell is well-positioned right now. The field is thick with decks that want to cast several
spells a turn — storm variants, the artifact-combo decks built around Mystic Forge and Emry, and
the cantrip-heavy blue decks — and Sphere of Resistance ("Spells cost {1} more to cast") attacks
exactly that plan. It is the same tax that has always punished storm, pointed at a whole tier of
the format at once.

---

## This deck's plan

**The kill.** Thespian's Stage copies Dark Depths. The copy enters with no ice counters (counters
aren't copied). The legend rule fires — keep the copy, put the original in the graveyard. The
copy's ability triggers: "When Dark Depths has no ice counters on it, sacrifice it. If you do,
create Marit Lage" — a legendary 20/20 flying indestructible token. Skateboard (equip {1}, "+1/+0
and haste") lets Marit Lage attack the same turn; it also taps a permanent when it enters, which
can clear a blocker or tap down a mana source on the swing turn.

**The backup.** Urza's Saga closes slower games with Construct tokens, and its chapter III fetches
an artifact costing {0} or {1} — Mox Diamond for mana, Expedition Map for the missing Depths piece,
or a silver bullet (Pithing Needle, Grafdigger's Cage, Soul-Guide Lantern) against the right
opponent. Crop Rotation and Expedition Map both assemble the combo or grab the exact utility land
the board is crying out for.

**The lock pieces.** Sphere taxes every spell they cast, and because our threats are lands, we pay
that tax far less than they do. Wasteland and Ghost Quarter attack a format where almost every
manabase is nonbasic. The Tabernacle at Pendrell Vale gives every creature "At the beginning of
your upkeep, destroy this creature unless you pay {1}" — with a Sphere alongside it, creature decks
are paying rent on two fronts, and Life from the Loam rebuys the Tabernacle through Wasteland wars.

**Playing with your own Sphere.** Sphere taxes you too — it hits Crop Rotation, Loam, and
Malevolent Rumble. Sequence your own spells the turn before Sphere comes down, or plan to play
lands-only through it. The deck that minds Sphere least wins the Sphere war, and with 26 lands and
a recursion engine, that's usually you. Yavimaya, Cradle of Growth makes every land a Forest, so
your green spells stay castable through your own tax and through a Blood Moon. Boseiju, Who Endures
and Otawara, Soaring City are Channel abilities ({1}{G} discard / {3}{U} discard), not casts, so
they dodge both counterspells and your own Sphere — Boseiju destroys an artifact, enchantment, or
nonbasic land (The One Ring, Chalice, Blood Moon, Sneak Attack, an Urza's Saga), and Otawara
bounces almost anything at instant speed.

**Karakas** is quietly one of the best cards in the deck. It returns any legendary creature to
hand — that means an opposing Marit Lage in the mirror, or a cheated-in Griselbrand, Atraxa, or
Emrakul, answered for a single tap, repeatably.

## Mulligan keep guide

The deck mulligans on a simple question: **does this hand deploy a relevant lock piece, or the
combo, by turn two — and can it survive their opening?** Seven cards that do nothing until turn
four are a ship no matter how many lands they hold.

You need two things almost every keep: enough mana to function (Ancient Tomb, Mox Diamond, and
Exploration all count as fast mana) and something that *does* something. Exploration hands want
three or more lands to snowball; a hand with Exploration and one land is a trap.

Read the keep against what you expect to face:

- **Against fair blue (Delver, Dimir, the Midrange decks):** you want Sphere plus the mana to cast
  it turn one, or a Wasteland-and-Loam start that grinds their manabase to nothing. A turn-one
  Sphere off Tomb or Mox is the dream — it taxes every Daze, Bolt, and cantrip they have. You can
  keep slower, land-heavy hands here because the game goes long and your lands outvalue theirs.
- **Against combo (Show and Tell, storm, Doomsday, the reanimator/graveyard decks):** speed and
  disruption are everything. Keep Sphere-on-one, a graveyard-hate opener (Bojuka Bog, Soul-Guide
  Lantern, or Cage), or a fast Depths draw that races them. Ship any seven that just durdles —
  no Sphere, no hate, no clock — because they will simply combo before you do anything.
- **Against creature aggro (Death & Taxes, Energy, the go-wide decks):** the Tabernacle is your
  best card and a hand that finds it is a strong keep. Drop of Honey (post-board) and Maze of Ith
  buy the time. Sphere is weaker here — their curve is low — so don't lean a keep on it alone.
- **Against the mirror and other prison decks (Lands, Eldrazi, Red Stompy):** you want to win the
  land war. Keep Wasteland-plus-Loam engines, a fast Depths kill, or Karakas to answer their Marit
  Lage. Against Red Stompy specifically, the Forest and a green source matter — they are how you
  cast Boseiju or Force of Vigor to break a Blood Moon.

Example keep: Ancient Tomb, Sphere of Resistance, Mox Diamond, Exploration, Wasteland, Loam, and a
land — deploys a turn-one Sphere, has a second lock piece, and grinds. Snap keep. Example ship:
three lands, Skateboard, Grafdigger's Cage, Maze of Ith, and Otawara against an unknown opponent —
no engine, no clock, no relevant tax. Mulligan it.

## Matchups & sideboard

Sample sizes on this specific build are small and the meta is young — the Candelabra of Tawnos ban
landed on June 29 — so treat every percentage as a lean, not a law. Each header cites the field
share and, where we have a record on this exact build, our win rate with the raw percentage and
sample size; where the build sample is missing the wider Lands data is quoted for context, and
where nothing reliable exists the read is purely mechanical.

Every sideboard plan below brings in exactly as many cards as it takes out and uses only the
fifteen in the board: 3 Disruptor Flute, 3 Force of Vigor, 2 Choke, 2 Dismember, 2 Endurance,
1 Drop of Honey, 1 Faerie Macabre, 1 Icetill Explorer.

#### 1. Tron (C) — 9.3% of the meta · ~57% for us (raw 71, n=7)
- **Their plan:** Grim Monolith and the Tron lands ramp into Karn, the Great Creator, Ugin, Eye of
  the Storms, The One Ring, and eventually Emrakul; Karn fetches artifacts and shuts off our own,
  Trinisphere taxes us back.
- **How to spot it:** the manabase gives it away instantly — Urza's Mine, Urza's Tower, and Urza's
  Power Plant are all 100%, with Ancient Tomb and Urza's Saga at 88% and a Planar Nexus for the
  colored splash. Early plays are Grim Monolith (90%), the Voltaic/Manifold Key untappers (83–87%),
  and Kozilek's Command (100%). If you see Candelabra of Tawnos it's a stale, pre-ban list.
- **Our plan:** Wasteland and Ghost Quarter break the Tron assembly — their mana lands are all
  nonbasic. Sphere taxes every artifact and planeswalker they cast, and Boseiju's Channel destroys
  The One Ring or a key rock without them being able to counter it. Trinisphere will tax our cheap
  spells, so favour land-based lines and race with Marit Lage before Emrakul arrives.
- **In:** 3 Force of Vigor (destroy Grim Monolith, The One Ring, Trinisphere on the cheap).
- **Out:** 1 Grafdigger's Cage, 1 Soul-Guide Lantern, 1 Bojuka Bog (no graveyard to attack here).
- The wider Lands data agrees at 59% (raw 67, n=18).

#### 2. Izzet Delver (UR) — 7.7% of the meta · ~61% for us (raw 86, n=7)
- **Their plan:** an eighteen-land tempo deck — Daze, Force of Will, Lightning Bolt, and a cheap
  clock (Dragon's Rage Channeler, Murktide Regent) backed by Brainstorm and Ponder.
- **How to spot it:** Volcanic Island (99%) plus Wasteland (100%) and a pile of blue fetches
  (Polluted Delta, Scalding Tarn, Flooded Strand, all ~91–95%); the early spells are unmistakable —
  Ponder, Lightning Bolt, Mishra's Bauble, Dragon's Rage Channeler, and Daze all at 100%, with Flow
  State (98%) and Cori-Steel Cutter (92%). It plays nearly identically to Izzet Midrange in the
  first few turns; treat them the same until a heavier midrange top-end shows up.
- **Our plan:** Sphere plus Wasteland is the whole game — every extra land they lose makes Daze and
  Force of Will dead, and Sphere taxes each cantrip and threat. Choke ("Islands don't untap during
  their controllers' untap steps") frequently just ends it. Tabernacle and Drop of Honey clean up
  Channeler and Murktide; Cori-Steel Cutter is an artifact if you'd rather Force of Vigor it.
- **In:** 2 Choke, 1 Drop of Honey (grinds their creatures), 1 Dismember (kills Murktide or a
  turn-one Channeler).
- **Out:** 1 Grafdigger's Cage, 1 Soul-Guide Lantern, 1 Bojuka Bog, 1 Skateboard.

#### 3. Show and Tell (UR) — 6.9% of the meta · ~40% for us (raw 31, n=16)
- **Their plan:** Show and Tell or Sneak Attack on turn one or two puts Emrakul, Atraxa, or an
  Omniscience into play, protected by Force of Will and Flusterstorm.
- **How to spot it:** Ancient Tomb (100%) alongside Island (95%) and blue fetches, with Brainstorm
  (98%), Ponder (95%), and Lotus Petal (94%) but no fair threats. To tell the two flavours apart,
  watch the colours: a red splash (Volcanic Island 88%, Scalding Tarn 91%) points to the Sneak
  Attack build, while a straight blue-heavy list leans on the Omniscience package — the sideboard
  targets differ, so try to bait out which one before committing your Flutes.
- **Our plan:** they go over the top of the lock, so we answer the threat directly — Karakas
  bounces Emrakul or Atraxa (both legendary) every turn, and Maze of Ith fogs an Emrakul attack.
  Disruptor Flute names Show and Tell (a {3} tax turns it into a six-drop) or shuts off Sneak
  Attack's ability outright; Pithing Needle also names Sneak Attack. Force of Vigor destroys a
  resolved Omniscience or Sneak Attack (both are enchantments). Race with Marit Lage when the hand
  offers it.
- **In:** 3 Disruptor Flute, 1 Force of Vigor.
- **Out:** 1 Grafdigger's Cage, 1 Soul-Guide Lantern, 1 Bojuka Bog, 1 Blast Zone (too slow here).
- The wider Lands data is a touch better at 45% (raw 45, n=123).

#### 4. Energy (WR) — 5.5% of the meta · ~50% for us (raw 50, n=6)
- **Their plan:** a white-red energy aggro deck — Guide of Souls and Ocelot Pride generate life,
  energy, and tokens, Ajani and Voice of Victory push damage, Goblin Bombardment is a sacrifice
  outlet, and Static Prison holds down a threat.
- **How to spot it:** Plains (99%) and Wasteland (99%) with Karakas (96%) and white fetches into
  Plateau; the opening plays are Guide of Souls and Ocelot Pride (both 100%), Voice of Victory
  (98%), Swords to Plowshares (97%), and Ajani, Nacatl Pariah (96%). A turn-one Guide of Souls is
  the signature.
- **Our plan:** The Tabernacle is brutal against a go-wide board — every token pays {1} each
  upkeep — and Drop of Honey grinds them down one creature at a time. Force of Vigor destroys
  Static Prison and Goblin Bombardment (both enchantments); Dismember kills the Guide of Souls that
  fuels the engine. Sphere matters less against this low curve, so don't over-rely on it.
- **In:** 1 Drop of Honey, 2 Dismember, 1 Force of Vigor.
- **Out:** 1 Grafdigger's Cage, 1 Soul-Guide Lantern, 1 Bojuka Bog, 1 Skateboard.
- The wider Lands data agrees at 53% (raw 54, n=48).

#### 5. Grixis Reanimator (UBR) — 5.1% of the meta · no reliable data — mechanical read
- **Their plan:** discard or loot a fatty (Faithless Looting, Unmask), then Reanimate, Animate Dead,
  or Shallow Grave it turn one or two — Griselbrand, Atraxa, or Archon of Cruelty takes over.
- **How to spot it:** Badlands and Underground Sea with Swamp (99%) and Polluted Delta point to the
  Grixis shell; the tells are Dark Ritual, Lotus Petal, Reanimate, and Animate Dead all at 100%,
  plus Faithless Looting (96%) and Shallow Grave (96%). Distinguish it from a Dimir deck that merely
  splashes Reanimate — this list is trying to cheat something out on turn one or two, not grind, so
  a fast Dark Ritual into Reanimate is the giveaway.
- **Our plan:** our graveyard hate is already maindeck. Grafdigger's Cage stops the reanimation
  target from entering (a creature card can't enter the battlefield from a graveyard), and Bojuka
  Bog and Soul-Guide Lantern exile the yard before they get there. Karakas bounces the reanimated
  legend repeatedly. Sphere taxes their Reanimate and rituals. Bring the rest of the package.
- **In:** 2 Endurance, 1 Faerie Macabre.
- **Out:** 1 Pithing Needle, 1 Skateboard, 1 Blast Zone.
- The wider Lands data reads unfavorable at 40% (raw 22, n=9), but that predates our maindeck
  graveyard hate — the mechanical read is closer to even.

#### 6. Blue Artifacts (U) — 4.6% of the meta · ~63% for us (raw 88, n=8)
- **Their plan:** fast artifact mana (Mox Opal, Lotus Petal) into Emry, Lurker of the Loch, which
  recasts artifacts from the graveyard, building toward a Pinnacle Emissary payoff.
- **How to spot it:** Urza's Saga (97%) with Seat of the Synod and Ancient Tomb, and openers of
  Lotus Petal (99%), Mox Opal (97%), Mishra's Bauble (82%), and Skateboard (76%). A turn-one Mox
  Opal into a bauble and an Emry is the pattern.
- **Our plan:** Force of Vigor blows up two artifacts for free, Sphere taxes their cheap spells and
  Emry, and — importantly — Grafdigger's Cage stops Emry casting artifacts out of the graveyard, so
  it stays in. The Tabernacle taxes Emry, Emissary, and their Constructs. Dismember kills Emry or a
  payoff.
- **In:** 3 Force of Vigor, 1 Dismember.
- **Out:** 1 Soul-Guide Lantern, 1 Bojuka Bog, 1 Maze of Ith, 1 Skateboard.
- The wider Lands data agrees at 54% (raw 55, n=60).

#### 7. Doomsday (UBG) — 4.4% of the meta · no reliable data — mechanical read
- **Their plan:** ritual mana (Dark Ritual, Lion's Eye Diamond, Lotus Petal) into Doomsday, then a
  five-card pile and a Thassa's Oracle win, all protected by Force of Will and Daze.
- **How to spot it:** Underground Sea and Polluted Delta (both 100%) with Cavern of Souls (99%) —
  the Cavern is there to force through Thassa's Oracle — and Island (95%). The openers are Thassa's
  Oracle, Lion's Eye Diamond, Brainstorm, Lotus Petal, Daze, and Dark Ritual, all at 100%. Cavern
  of Souls plus rituals and no fair clock is the tell.
- **Our plan:** Sphere taxes every ritual, cantrip, Doomsday itself, and the Oracle — it slows the
  whole chain. Choke strands their blue mana, and Disruptor Flute names Doomsday for a {3} tax or
  Thassa's Oracle to make the payoff a five-drop. Then race with Marit Lage. Graveyard hate does
  little here, so it comes out.
- **In:** 2 Choke, 3 Disruptor Flute.
- **Out:** 1 Grafdigger's Cage, 1 Soul-Guide Lantern, 1 Bojuka Bog, 1 Maze of Ith, 1 Skateboard.
- The wider Lands data reads unfavorable at 45% (raw 44, n=50).

#### 8. Lands (G) — 4.2% of the meta · ~50% for us (raw 50, n=32, across Lands decks)
- **Their plan:** the more common Lands build runs Once Upon a Time for consistency and leans less
  on Sphere — otherwise the same engine we play: Exploration, Loam, Wasteland, Depths + Stage,
  Tabernacle, and Urza's Saga. Treat this as the mirror.
- **How to spot it:** Dark Depths, Boseiju, Urza's Saga, Karakas, Wasteland, and Maze of Ith all at
  100%, with Life from the Loam, Mox Diamond, and Exploration (all 100%) plus Crop Rotation (98%).
  If you see Once Upon a Time and fewer Spheres, it's the common midrange-lands build rather than a
  prison mirror.
- **Our plan:** our four Spheres are the edge — they tax the opponent's Crop Rotation, Loam, and
  Rumble while our lands-first plan minds the tax less. The game is a land war: Wasteland and Ghost
  Quarter fight over their Depths, Stage, and Tabernacle, Karakas bounces their Marit Lage, and
  Maze of Ith fogs it if it connects. Pithing Needle can name Thespian's Stage to shut off their
  combo line. Icetill Explorer gives extra land drops and recurs lands from the yard to out-grind.
- **In:** 1 Icetill Explorer, 1 Force of Vigor (pops their Exploration, Mox, or Sphere).
- **Out:** 1 Grafdigger's Cage (nothing to stop), 1 Skateboard.

#### 9. Dimir Tempo (UB) — 4.1% of the meta · ~69% for us (raw 88, n=16)
- **Their plan:** Thoughtseize and Fatal Push clear the way, Orcish Bowmasters and Murktide (plus
  Kaito and a flipped Tamiyo) provide the clock, and Force of Will, Daze, and Snuff Out protect it.
- **How to spot it:** Underground Sea and Wasteland (both 100%) with Undercity Sewers, Polluted
  Delta, and Swamp — straight two-colour UB — and openers of Thoughtseize, Brainstorm, Ponder, and
  Fatal Push (all 100%), with Tamiyo (95%), Orcish Bowmasters (95%), and Daze (90%). The presence of
  Thoughtseize + Bowmasters over pure Delver cantrips separates it from Dimir Delver.
- **Our plan:** the same story as Delver with a slower clock, and it's our best fair matchup — their
  removal can't touch our lands, and Sphere plus Wasteland grinds their disruption into nothing.
  Choke locks their Islands; Tabernacle and Drop of Honey handle Bowmasters, Murktide, and Kaito.
  Push through their Thoughtseize with sheer redundancy.
- **In:** 2 Choke, 1 Drop of Honey, 1 Dismember.
- **Out:** 1 Grafdigger's Cage, 1 Soul-Guide Lantern, 1 Bojuka Bog, 1 Skateboard.
- The wider Lands data confirms it at 64% (raw 66, n=125).

#### 10. Death & Taxes (W) — 3.5% of the meta · ~43% for us (raw 29, n=7)
- **Their plan:** Aether Vial out hatebears, Stoneforge Mystic fetches Meteor Sword or Batterskull,
  Karakas answers our Marit Lage, and Swords to Plowshares plus a low curve shrug off Sphere;
  Skyclave Apparition exiles our artifacts.
- **How to spot it:** Karakas (100%) and Wasteland (99%) over Plains, with Witch Enchanter (99%) and
  Flagstones of Trokair; the openers are Swords to Plowshares and Aether Vial (both 100%), Phelia
  (95%), Stoneforge Mystic (93%), and often a turn-one Thalia (61%). A turn-one Aether Vial or
  Thalia into hatebears is the signature.
- **Our plan:** The Tabernacle is the whole matchup — every hatebear pays rent each upkeep — and
  Drop of Honey grinds behind it. Force of Vigor is superb here: it destroys Aether Vial, Batterskull,
  Meteor Sword, or Lion Sash. Dismember kills Stoneforge or Recruiter. Because their Karakas
  neutralises Marit Lage, lean on the Tabernacle grind, Urza's Saga Constructs, and Ghost Quarter on
  their Karakas. Sphere is weak against their one-drops and Vial, so trim it.
- **In:** 1 Drop of Honey, 2 Dismember, 3 Force of Vigor.
- **Out:** 2 Sphere of Resistance, 1 Grafdigger's Cage, 1 Soul-Guide Lantern, 1 Bojuka Bog,
  1 Skateboard.
- The wider Lands data is a touch better at 48% (raw 47, n=57).

**Budget note.** The Tabernacle at Pendrell Vale is the price tag, and this build runs it as a
one-of by choice, not compromise. Mox Diamond and the Depths package are the rest of the cost.

**A note on the numbers.** Every percentage here is a small, recent sample against a meta that's
only a couple of weeks into the post-Candelabra world. Some pairings have a handful of games and
some have none at all — where the sample is thin the honest answer is the mechanical read, not the
win rate. Expect the leans to move as the field settles, and trust the interaction (what Sphere
taxes, what Wasteland strips, what your hate names) over any single decimal.
