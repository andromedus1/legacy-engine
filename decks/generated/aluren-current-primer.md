# Aluren — current online primer

Generated from 129 in-window decks (`established`) over the archetype's stable window beginning
2026-03-16, then refreshed against the current online/MTGO field through 2026-07-30. The current
archetype is a hybrid combo shell: the refresh made no maindeck swaps and supplied the recommended
sideboard in the paired Moxfield-import file.

## Plan

This is not the older fair-creature Aluren deck. It has two overlapping ways to go over the top:
Aluren plus Acererak the Archlich, and Show and Tell plus Omniscience or Atraxa, Grand Unifier.
Ancient Tomb and Lotus Petal accelerate both plans; Brainstorm, Ponder, and Stock Up find the right
half; Force of Will and Veil of Summer protect the critical turn.

Mulligan for a coherent engine rather than a pile of interchangeable expensive cards. Decide early
whether the hand is an Aluren hand or a Show and Tell hand, but preserve the ability to pivot when
the opponent reveals narrow hate. Be careful about exposing a Show and Tell line when the opponent
can put in a stronger permanent.

## Sideboard map

- **Blue decks:** Carpet of Flowers, Defense Grid, and Force of Negation support protected setup and
  combo turns.
- **Colorless/permanent hate:** Consign to Memory, Force of Vigor, Nature's Claim, Engineered
  Explosives, and Pithing Needle provide varied answers.
- **Graveyards:** Ghost Vacuum, Heritage Reclamation, and Soul-Guide Lantern form a compact graveyard
  package.
- **Red threats:** Blue Elemental Blast is the dedicated red interaction slot.

The current matchup-specific OUT/IN suggestions did not clear the per-card confidence gate. The
engine labels its guidance against Dimir Reanimator, Dimir Tempo, Eldrazi, Show and Tell, Oops! All
Spells, Doomsday, Izzet Delver, and Mystic Forge as reasoning-based. Use the sideboard roles to
answer the hate actually shown and avoid diluting both combo engines simultaneously.

Per-card win rates are presence-correlational, not causal. The shell is established, but its hybrid
identity makes old Aluren heuristics unreliable.
