# Grixis Reanimator — current online primer

Generated from 234 in-window decks (`established`) over the archetype's stable window beginning
2026-05-11, then refreshed against the current online/MTGO field through 2026-07-30. The refresh
made no maindeck swaps and supplied the recommended sideboard in the paired Moxfield-import file.

## Plan

Grixis Reanimator converts discard outlets and self-discard into an early Griselbrand, Atraxa,
Archon of Cruelty, or Raph & Mikey. Faithless Looting, Unmask, Cabal Therapy, Collective Brutality,
and discard aimed at yourself stock the graveyard; Reanimate, Animate Dead, and Shallow Grave bring
the threat back. Dark Ritual and Lotus Petal let the deck compress setup, protection, and reanimation
into one turn.

Mulligan for a complete sequence: a threat to bin, a reanimation effect, mana, and enough disruption
for the matchup. Against open interaction, Thoughtseize/Unmask before committing. Shallow Grave lines
reward careful combat and timing; Reanimate lines require life-total discipline.

## Sideboard map

- **Blue interaction:** Defense Grid is the main protection package; Duress and Collective Brutality
  add discard.
- **Artifacts/prison:** Abrade and Meltdown answer artifact-heavy hate and boards.
- **Creatures:** Fatal Push and Force of Despair cover different creature pressures.
- **Fast combo/graveyards:** Mindbreak Trap, Leyline of the Void, and Dauthi Voidwalker provide
  narrow high-impact interaction.
- **Acceleration/pivot support:** Ancient Tomb helps deploy expensive sideboard plans and Defense
  Grid quickly.

The current matchup-specific OUT/IN suggestions did not clear the per-card confidence gate. The
engine labels its guidance against Dimir Reanimator, Dimir Tempo, Eldrazi, Show and Tell, Oops! All
Spells, Doomsday, Izzet Delver, and Mystic Forge as reasoning-based. Do not over-sideboard: preserve
enough threats, discard outlets, reanimation effects, and mana for a functioning opening hand.

Per-card win rates are presence-correlational, not causal. Revisit the sideboard if graveyard hate or
fast combo changes materially.
