## Plan

ANT is a ritual-and-tutor storm deck. Use Brainstorm and Ponder to assemble mana, protection, and a
payoff; use Thoughtseize, Duress, and Veil of Summer to create a safe turn; then convert Dark Ritual,
Cabal Ritual, Lotus Petal, Chrome Mox, and Lion's Eye Diamond into a lethal Tendrils of Agony.
Ad Nauseam, Beseech the Mirror, Infernal Tutor, and Gaea's Will give the deck several ways to turn a
resource-heavy hand or graveyard into the winning chain.

Mulligan toward a hand with a credible route to both mana and a payoff. Against blue interaction,
value a protected turn over raw speed; against noninteractive decks, prioritize the shortest stable
line. Track life total carefully before committing to Ad Nauseam.

## Sideboard map

- **Blue tempo/control:** Carpet of Flowers and Defense Grid provide the main anti-permission plan;
  Spell Pierce is an additional stack-interaction option.
- **Colorless permanents and prison pieces:** Consign to Memory, Engineered Explosives, Abrupt Decay,
  and Boseiju provide overlapping answers.
- **Graveyard/combo:** Dauthi Voidwalker is the dedicated graveyard slot; discard remains the primary
  maindeck interaction.
- **Creature-heavy boards:** Engineered Explosives is the broad reset.
