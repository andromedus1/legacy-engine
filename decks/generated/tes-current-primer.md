## Plan

TES is a fast, wish-driven storm deck. Its artifact mana and rituals create explosive turns;
Burning Wish, Beseech the Mirror, Gamble, Echo of Eons, Gaea's Will, Song of Creation, and the
sideboard payoffs provide overlapping conversion routes. Sequence cantrips and setup effects to
preserve the best payoff while counting both mana and storm. Burning Wish means sideboard cards can
also be part of the combo toolbox, so avoid boarding away a target your planned line requires.

Keep hands with a credible mana engine plus selection or payoff. Against permission, build toward a
Defense Grid turn rather than exposing the key spell too early. Against other fast combo decks,
speed and the single pieces of discard or stack interaction matter more than grinding.

## Sideboard map

- **Blue interaction:** four Defense Grid are the primary protection package.
- **Permanent hate:** Boseiju, Boomerang Basics, Shattering Spree, and Massacre cover different
  permanent classes.
- **Fast combo/graveyard:** Thoughtseize, Mindbreak Trap, and Leyline of the Void are the compact
  interaction package.
- **Wish/conversion targets:** Beseech the Mirror, Empty the Warrens, and Peer into the Abyss retain
  alternate routes when Tendrils is awkward.
