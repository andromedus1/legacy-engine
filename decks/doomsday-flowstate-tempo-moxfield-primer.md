# Doomsday — Flow State Tempo

The current shape of Legacy Doomsday. Roughly 85% of Doomsday lists in the post-May metagame have
adopted Flow State, and the consensus build that emerged is tempo-configured: a fair blue-black
shell (Tamiyo, Murktide, Wasteland, full countermagic) that happens to kill with one card. Play it
like a Dimir tempo deck that has a combo finish in its back pocket, not like a combo deck that
happens to run counterspells.

---

## This deck's plan

**Flow State** ({1}{U}, sorcery): "Look at the top three cards of your library. Put one of them
into your hand and the rest on the bottom of your library in any order. If there is an instant card
and a sorcery card in your graveyard, instead put two of them into your hand." In a twenty-cantrip
deck with fetchlands, it's drawing two by turn two — Impulse-grade selection without Lion's Eye
Diamond risk. It's the reason this generation of the deck exists.

**One LED, not four.** This build keeps a single Lion's Eye Diamond. The deck kills fine at sorcery
speed through open mana most games; the LED copy enables the protected instant-speed kill when it
turns up. It also cuts the deck's price dramatically.

**Sixty percent of your games are fair.** Tamiyo, Murktide, Wasteland, three Thoughtseize and a
full countersuite mean you can win a grind you never comboed in. Don't force Doomsday when the fair
plan is winning — the combo is a finisher, not a mandate.

### The kill

Learn two piles first; the rest are refinements.

**The basic pile** (turn 3+, Doomsday plus about three mana): cast Doomsday, stack
[Thassa's Oracle, Consider, Street Wraith, protection, filler]. Draw into the pile with Consider and
Street Wraith (cycling costs 2 life, not mana — free draws through a pile), cast Oracle, win on the
trigger. Cavern of Souls naming Wizard makes Oracle uncounterable.

**The protected pile** (LED in play): stack the pile so LED cracks in response to interaction on
Oracle. Practice holding priority here — it's where new Doomsday pilots leak wins.

Edge of Autumn cycles by sacrificing a land; after Doomsday resolves you rarely need the fourth
land, so it's nearly always a free draw late.

**Pile-play notes you'll reach for in the matchups below.** Against Orcish Bowmasters, every draw
past the first in a step pings you and grows their Orc Army, so a Bowmasters-proof pile wins the
same turn it fires rather than chaining draws across turns. Against soft counters like Flusterstorm
you want a pile that still wins if Doomsday or Oracle eats one — lean on the LED line and hold up a
Force. And build Wasteland-proof: sequence so the kill doesn't hinge on a single fragile land they
can blow up in response.

## Mulligan keep guide

The question is never "do I have Doomsday." It's "does this hand do something on both axes — does it
disrupt, and does it close." A hand that only disrupts loses to their second wave; a hand that only
goldfishes loses to one counterspell.

**The floor.** Two or three lands with a blue source and at least one cantrip. Below that you're
hoping, and this deck digs well enough that you should ship hands that need two perfect draws.

**Against fair blue (Delver, the Dimir decks, the Azorius/Jeskai piles).** These games go long, so
you can keep slower. What you want is interaction plus a threat: Thoughtseize or Force of Will to
clear the way, and a Tamiyo, a Murktide, or the combo to actually end it. A hand of two lands,
Tamiyo, Ponder, and a Force is a fine keep with no Doomsday in sight — the fair shell wins that
game.

**Against combo (Show and Tell, the storm decks, Reanimator, Oops).** You need disruption *and* a
clock. Thoughtseize or Duress plus a Force, backed by something that closes before they rebuild —
the pile, a Murktide, or a Tamiyo. Pure disruption with no finish lets them draw back into it.
Ship hands that Thoughtseize once and then do nothing.

**Against creature decks (Energy, Death & Taxes, Red Stompy, the Eldrazi aggro draws).** Life total
and speed matter. Prize early combo or, post-board, cheap removal. Be stingier with Thoughtseize and
Street Wraith here — paying life into a clock is how you lose the race you were winning.

**When to keep a no-Doomsday hand.** Often. Roughly six in ten games you win with the fair deck, so
Tamiyo plus cantrips plus counters is a keep, full stop. Don't mulligan a functional tempo hand
chasing a combo piece.

**Example keeps:** Underground Sea, Polluted Delta, Ponder, Thoughtseize, Force of Will, Tamiyo —
snap keep, does everything. Island, Delta, Brainstorm, Doomsday, Dark Ritual, Lotus Petal — keep,
a real combo turn with selection to protect it.

**Example ships:** Doomsday, LED, Street Wraith, Consider, and one land — one land is not enough to
combo and the hand does nothing fair. Three lands, Murktide, Murktide, Edge of Autumn — no early
play, no disruption, all top-end.

## Matchups & sideboard

The sideboard's centerpiece is a four-of Barrowgoyf transformation. Barrowgoyf is a {2}{B} Lhurgoyf
with deathtouch and lifelink whose power equals the number of card types across *all* graveyards and
whose toughness is that number plus one — in a fetch-and-cantrip mirror the yards fill fast, so it's
routinely a 5/6-or-bigger deathtouch lifelinker that blocks anything and races anything. Against the
fair blue-black decks you side into a graveyard-fueled grind: they board their spot removal *out*
against a combo deck, your Barrowgoyfs land unanswered, and the games you couldn't combo you simply
out-size. Note the one tension — Murktide delves (exiles) instants and sorceries from your yard,
which shrinks Barrowgoyf's fuel; in the heaviest grind plans it's fine to lean on one threat or the
other.

Percentages are field share. Win rates cite the broader Doomsday deck as a baseline and the previous
generation of this tempo build where a sample exists — see the closing note.

#### 1. Tron (C) — 9.3% of the meta
**Win rate:** broader Doomsday ~61% (raw 71, n=17) — favorable but a thin sample.

**Their plan:** assemble the Urza lands plus Ancient Tomb into Karn, the Great Creator, The One
Ring, Ugin, and eventually Emrakul, locking you out with Trinisphere and Pithing Needle along the
way.

**How to spot it:** the Urza lands (Mine/Tower/Power Plant, all 100%) plus Planar Nexus and Ancient
Tomb, into Grim Monolith and Kozilek's Command. Unmistakable turn one. (Candelabra of Tawnos was
banned June 29, so the old fast-mana engine is gone and you'll rarely face this deck now.)

**Our plan:** race with the combo behind protection and blow up an Urza piece with Wasteland to buy
turns. Consign to Memory is gold here — it counters colorless spells (Karn, Ugin, Kozilek's Command)
and can counter The One Ring's enter-the-battlefield protection trigger. Duress and Force of Negation
take or stop the noncreature bombs. Barrowgoyf does nothing relevant — they have no removal to dodge
and no clock you need to block, so stay on the combo.

**In:** 2 Force of Negation, 1 Consign to Memory, 1 Duress (interaction for their bombs).
**Out:** 2 Daze (dead against Ancient Tomb mana), 2 Murktide Regent (too slow, outclassed by Ugin).

#### 2. Izzet Delver (UR) — 7.7% of the meta
**Win rate:** broader Doomsday ~42% (raw 40, n=109); previous tempo generation ~41% (raw 37, n=35).
The structural weakness — dead Dark Rituals against a deck of one-mana interaction, and no build of
Doomsday has fixed it.

**Their plan:** Dragon's Rage Channeler and a Cori-Steel Cutter token under Daze, Force of Will,
Lightning Bolt and Murktide — tempo you out while you fumble for a combo turn.

**How to spot it:** Volcanic Island and Wasteland into Bolt, Mishra's Bauble, DRC and Daze. This is
the Flow State generation of Delver (Flow State at 98%), so it grinds harder than the classic
Delver-heavy version — expect card advantage, not just a turn-one one-drop.

**Our plan:** don't try to out-tempo them; play the fair game and win the Murktide war with
Barrowgoyf plus removal. Their Bolt bounces off a five-toughness Barrowgoyf and their Murktide dies
to its deathtouch on the block.

**In:** 4 Barrowgoyf, 2 Force of Negation, 2 Fatal Push.
**Out:** 3 Thoughtseize (life you can't spend into their clock), 2 Lotus Petal, 1 Lion's Eye
Diamond, 1 Edge of Autumn, 1 Street Wraith (combo fuel you're de-emphasizing).

#### 3. Show and Tell (UR) — 6.9% of the meta
**Win rate:** broader Doomsday ~57% (raw 58, n=115); previous tempo generation ~56% (raw 59, n=39).

**Their plan:** Show and Tell or Sneak Attack a turn-one Emrakul or Atraxa and end the game before
you set up.

**How to spot it:** Ancient Tomb (100%) is the giveaway, plus a light blue cantrip base. Identify the
version in game one — Omniscience and Stock Up means the Omni-Tell build that wants a counter war;
Sneak Attack means the deck that just wants to be raced. If you see neither yet, play around both.

**Our plan:** Force of Negation stops Show and Tell and Sneak Attack (both noncreature). Consign to
Memory can't touch Show and Tell itself (a colored spell) but does counter Atraxa's
enter-the-battlefield draw trigger, denying them the refill. Against Omni-Tell, hold counters and
grind; against Sneak & Show, race with the combo.

**In:** 1 Consign to Memory, 2 Force of Negation, 1 Duress.
**Out:** 2 Murktide Regent, 1 Daze, 1 Wasteland (few targets, and you want spells not tempo).

#### 4. Energy (WR) — 5.5% of the meta
**Win rate:** broader Doomsday ~57% (raw 60, n=47); previous tempo generation ~49% (raw 47, n=19).

**Their plan:** a go-wide white aggro deck — Ocelot Pride and Guide of Souls build a lifegain-and-
tokens engine, Ajani and Voice of Victory push damage, Swords to Plowshares clears blockers.

**How to spot it:** Plains and Karakas alongside Wasteland, into Guide of Souls, Ocelot Pride and
Swords on turn one. White one-drops that gain life are the tell.

**Our plan:** race with the combo and use Barrowgoyf as the best blocker you own — deathtouch and
lifelink eats their go-wide starts and claws back the life their engines built. It's not the full
grind transformation, since their Swords stays live against it, but it's the stabilizer this matchup
wants. Fatal Push kills Guide of Souls and Ocelot Pride to stop the over-the-top draws.

**In:** 4 Barrowgoyf, 2 Fatal Push.
**Out:** 2 Daze (weak against a creature deck), 2 Thoughtseize and 1 Street Wraith (stop paying life
into their clock), 1 Consider.

#### 5. Grixis Reanimator (UBR) — 5.1% of the meta
**Win rate:** broader Doomsday ~56% (raw 59, n=27) — favorable, smaller sample.

**Their plan:** Reanimate or Animate Dead a Griselbrand or Atraxa off a turn-one discard outlet,
powered by Dark Ritual, Faithless Looting and Shallow Grave.

**How to spot it:** Badlands and Raucous Theater — the red is the tell for *this* flavor — into Dark
Ritual, Reanimate, Animate Dead, Faithless Looting and Shallow Grave. If you see Badlands and red
looting effects, it's Grixis Reanimator, not a pure Dimir reanimator shell.

**Our plan:** disrupt and race. Thoughtseize and Duress take the reanimation spell or the fatty;
Force of Negation counters Reanimate and Animate Dead. Dauthi Voidwalker is the key card — it exiles
their reanimation targets as they hit the yard and clocks them behind shadow.

**In:** 2 Dauthi Voidwalker, 2 Force of Negation, 1 Duress.
**Out:** 2 Murktide Regent (too slow for the race), 1 Consider, 1 Street Wraith, 1 Edge of Autumn.

#### 6. Blue Artifacts (U) — 4.6% of the meta
**Win rate:** broader Doomsday ~43% (raw 42, n=76); previous tempo generation ~47% (raw 45, n=33).
Slightly unfavorable.

**Their plan:** Urza's Saga and Mox-powered artifact aggro — Emry recurs cheap artifacts, Skateboard
and Pinnacle Emissary carry the beats, all deployed a turn ahead of schedule off fast mana.

**How to spot it:** Urza's Saga (97%) plus Seat of the Synod and Ancient Tomb, into Mox Opal, Lotus
Petal and Mishra's Bauble. Fast colorless-ish mana with a blue splash.

**Our plan:** side into the grind — Barrowgoyf out-sizes their whole board and Fatal Push kills Emry
(with a fetch cracked) and their carriers. Keep two Doomsday so the combo threat keeps them honest.

**In:** 4 Barrowgoyf, 2 Fatal Push.
**Out:** 2 Daze (weak into Ancient Tomb), 1 Lotus Petal, 1 Lion's Eye Diamond, 1 Street Wraith, 1
Edge of Autumn.

#### 7. Doomsday (UBG) — 4.4% of the meta
**Win rate:** broader Doomsday ~50% (raw 50, n=26) — an even mirror, small sample.

**Their plan:** the same deck — whoever resolves Doomsday first behind protection, or strips the
other's defense, usually wins.

**How to spot it:** Underground Sea, Cavern of Souls and Undercity Sewers into Thassa's Oracle, LED
and the cantrip suite. To read the generation: Tamiyo and Flow State means the fair tempo build like
yours; a heavier LED/Petal count with no Tamiyo means an older pure-combo build that will try to
combo faster and lighter on interaction.

**Our plan:** win the disruption fight, not a speed race. Thoughtseize and Duress take their Force or
Doomsday. The marquee tech: Consign to Memory counters Thassa's Oracle's win trigger — let them
"combo," then fizzle the Oracle and keep the game going.

**In:** 2 Force of Negation, 1 Consign to Memory, 1 Duress.
**Out:** 2 Murktide Regent (irrelevant in a combo mirror), 1 Wasteland, 1 Consider.

#### 8. Lands (G) — 4.2% of the meta
**Win rate:** broader Doomsday ~55% (raw 56, n=50); previous tempo generation ~52% (raw 55, n=11).

**Their plan:** Dark Depths for a 20/20, or grind with Life from the Loam plus Wasteland while
Sphere of Resistance taxes you and Maze of Ith and Karakas neutralize attackers.

**How to spot it:** Dark Depths, Boseiju, Maze of Ith and Urza's Saga into Exploration, Mox Diamond,
Crop Rotation and Loam. An all-land deck with no real spells.

**Our plan:** combo through them — they have almost no countermagic. Thoughtseize and Duress take
Sphere or Crop Rotation; Force of Negation stops their key noncreature piece; Consign counters
Sphere of Resistance (a colorless spell) on the stack. Don't bother with Barrowgoyf — Maze and
Karakas blank attackers.

**In:** 2 Force of Negation, 1 Duress, 1 Consign to Memory.
**Out:** 2 Murktide Regent (taxed and blanked), 1 Daze, 1 Consider.

#### 9. Dimir Tempo (UB) — 4.1% of the meta
**Win rate:** broader Doomsday ~46% (raw 46, n=123); previous tempo generation ~50% (raw 50, n=48).
The transformation matchup — the reason the sideboard is built this way.

**Their plan:** Thoughtseize and Orcish Bowmasters strip and punish, Fatal Push and Snuff Out remove,
Tamiyo and Kaito and Murktide carry a fair clock.

**How to spot it:** Underground Sea, Wasteland and Undercity Sewers into Thoughtseize, Fatal Push,
Bowmasters and Tamiyo. Bowmasters plus Fatal Push in a two-color UB shell is the tell versus the
grindier Dimir Midrange.

**Our plan:** they board their removal *out* against your combo, so your Barrowgoyfs land unanswered
and out-size Bowmasters tokens and the Orc Army; deathtouch-lifelink races Kaito and Murktide. Keep
two Doomsday — the threat of the combo keeps their remaining counters awkward. If you do combo, watch
Bowmasters: build the pile to win the same turn rather than chaining draws that ping you.

**In:** 4 Barrowgoyf, 2 Fatal Push, 1 Bitter Triumph.
**Out:** 2 Doomsday, 2 Street Wraith, 1 Edge of Autumn, 1 Lion's Eye Diamond, 1 Lotus Petal.

#### 10. Death & Taxes (W) — 3.5% of the meta
**Win rate:** broader Doomsday ~69% (raw 76, n=41) — strongly favorable; previous tempo generation
~56% (raw 64, n=11).

**Their plan:** Aether Vial and a hatebear toolbox — Thalia taxes, Stoneforge fetches Batterskull,
Solitude and Swords and Skyclave pick off your permanents. This is the taxes build without Spheres,
so your Rituals still work.

**How to spot it:** Karakas and Wasteland with Plains, into Aether Vial, Swords, Stoneforge and
Phelia. No Chalice or Sphere means your fast mana is live.

**Our plan:** just combo — their interaction is aimed at creatures, not at a pile, and they have no
counters. Thassa's Oracle isn't legendary, so Karakas can't touch the kill. Cheap removal handles
the taxers: Long Goodbye kills Thalia or Stoneforge uncounterably, Fatal Push and Bitter Triumph mop
up. Skip Barrowgoyf — they keep a pile of removal that answers it.

**In:** 2 Fatal Push, 1 Bitter Triumph, 1 Long Goodbye.
**Out:** 2 Daze (weak against Vial and a fair creature deck), 1 Consider, 1 Flow State.

#### 11. Dimir Midrange (UB) — 2.9% of the meta
**Win rate:** broader Doomsday ~51% (raw 52, n=27); previous tempo generation ~48% (raw 44, n=9,
thin).

**Their plan:** a grindier Dimir deck — Thoughtseize and Force of Will up front, a small Reanimate
package for a haymaker, Bowmasters and removal to trade one-for-one.

**How to spot it:** Underground Sea and Undercity Sewers with a green splash, into Thoughtseize and
Flow State but lighter on Ponder and Daze than the tempo build, plus the occasional Reanimate. Fewer
Tamiyo, more raw card advantage.

**Our plan:** the transformation applies — they trim removal against the combo, and Barrowgoyf grinds
past everything they keep. Duress takes their Force or a Reanimate.

**In:** 4 Barrowgoyf, 2 Fatal Push, 1 Duress.
**Out:** 2 Doomsday, 2 Street Wraith, 1 Edge of Autumn, 1 Lion's Eye Diamond, 1 Lotus Petal.

#### 12. Azorius Midrange (WU) — 2.7% of the meta
**Win rate:** broader Doomsday ~56% (raw 62, n=16) — favorable, thin sample.

**Their plan:** a Phelia-and-Quantum-Riddler tempo deck — flash creatures and card advantage behind
Swords, Force of Will and Teferi, Time Raveler, which shuts off your instant-speed plays.

**How to spot it:** Tundra and Meticulous Archive into Swords, Tamiyo, Phelia and Quantum Riddler.
The white-blue two-color base with Teferi is the tell.

**Our plan:** side into the grind — Swords gets worse against a combo deck, so Barrowgoyf sticks.
Long Goodbye is the key answer: it's uncounterable and kills Teferi (mana value three), so their
Force and Force of Negation can't protect it. Duress clears a counter before you commit.

**In:** 4 Barrowgoyf, 1 Long Goodbye, 1 Duress.
**Out:** 2 Doomsday, 2 Street Wraith, 1 Edge of Autumn, 1 Lotus Petal.

#### 13. Jeskai Midrange (WU) — 2.6% of the meta
**Win rate:** broader Doomsday ~52% (raw 53, n=38); previous tempo generation ~63% (raw 82, n=11,
favorable, small).

**Their plan:** a control-midrange with Swords, Prismatic Ending, Force of Negation and sweepers —
Wrath of the Skies and Forth Eorlingas — closing with Murktide.

**How to spot it:** Volcanic Island, Tundra and Flooded Strand into Swords, Prismatic Ending and Flow
State. Three colors with sweepers and Dress Down, not just spot removal.

**Our plan:** grind, but respect the sweepers — Wrath of the Skies and Dress Down punish a wide
Barrowgoyf board, so deploy them one at a time into open mana. Jace, Wielder of Mysteries is your
sweeper-proof win: a planeswalker they can't Swords, with a draw-from-empty finish that ends grindy
games. Duress protects the plan.

**In:** 4 Barrowgoyf, 1 Jace, Wielder of Mysteries, 1 Duress.
**Out:** 2 Doomsday, 2 Street Wraith, 1 Edge of Autumn, 1 Lotus Petal.

#### 14. Eldrazi (C) — 2.3% of the meta
**Win rate:** broader Doomsday ~44% (raw 43, n=77); previous tempo generation ~45% (raw 42, n=26).
Slightly unfavorable.

**Their plan:** Ancient Tomb and Eldrazi Temple into a turn-two Thought-Knot Seer or Reality Smasher,
with Chalice of the Void on one shutting off your entire one-mana engine.

**How to spot it:** Cavern of Souls, Eldrazi Temple and Eye of Ugin into Chalice and Kozilek's
Command. Colorless fast mana with big creatures.

**Our plan:** Chalice on one hoses your cantrips and Rituals, so lean fair rather than forcing the
combo. Consign to Memory is your best card — it counters their colorless creature spells and, better,
counters Thought-Knot Seer's enter trigger to save your hand. Force of Negation stops Chalice and
Kozilek's Command. Barrowgoyf blocks Reality Smasher and Thought-Knot behind deathtouch, and it
dodges Chalice at three mana.

**In:** 4 Barrowgoyf, 1 Consign to Memory, 2 Force of Negation.
**Out:** 2 Daze, 2 Doomsday (hard to combo under Chalice), 1 Lotus Petal, 1 Lion's Eye Diamond,
1 Consider.

#### 15. TES (UBRG) — 2.2% of the meta
**Win rate:** broader Doomsday ~59% (raw 67, n=18) — favorable, small sample.

**Their plan:** The EPIC Storm — Rituals and Lion's Eye Diamond into Beseech the Mirror or Burning

**How to spot it:** Badlands and Bloodstained Mire into Chrome Mox, LED, Dark Ritual, Burning Wish

**In:** 2 Force of Negation, 1 Consign to Memory, 1 Duress.
**Out:** 2 Murktide Regent (too slow), 1 Wasteland, 1 Consider.

#### 17. Cradle Control (WUBG) — 1.5% of the meta
**Win rate:** broader Doomsday ~35% (raw 27, n=26) — clearly unfavorable; previous tempo generation

**Their plan:** ramp on Gaea's Cradle and Ignoble Hierarch into Green Sun's Zenith and Natural Order

**How to spot it:** Gaea's Cradle, Bayou and Dryad Arbor into Green Sun's Zenith, Ignoble Hierarch

**Our plan:** combo fast before they set up, and disrupt the pieces that hurt you most. Thoughtseize

**In:** 2 Force of Negation, 1 Duress, 1 Long Goodbye.
**Out:** 2 Murktide Regent, 1 Daze, 1 Consider.

#### 18. Dredge (UBRG) — 1.4% of the meta

**How to spot it:** Ancient Tomb and Urza's Saga into Grim Monolith, Voltaic Key, Mox Opal and The
One Ring. Colorless artifact ramp with a white splash.

**Our plan:** disrupt and race. Consign to Memory is excellent — The One Ring, Grim Monolith, Mystic
Forge and Kozilek's Command are all colorless spells it can counter. Duress and Thoughtseize take
Mystic Forge or the Ring; Force of Negation backs it up.

**In:** 2 Force of Negation, 1 Consign to Memory, 1 Duress.
**Out:** 2 Murktide Regent, 2 Daze (weak into Ancient Tomb mana).

#### 23. Golgari Landfall (BG) — 1.1% of the meta

#### 24. Oops! All Spells (UB) — 1.1% of the meta
**Win rate:** broader Doomsday ~62% (raw 67, n=45) — favorable; previous tempo generation ~57%
(raw 67, n=12).

**Their plan:** Balustrade Spy or Undercity Informer mills their own library, then Dread Return or
Thassa's Oracle wins on the spot — often turn one or two.

**How to spot it:** Agadeem's Awakening and Boggart Trawler (a zero-land manabase feel) into Lotus

**Our plan:** hate and race. Dauthi Voidwalker exiles their milled library so there's nothing to

with it much.

**In:** 2 Dauthi Voidwalker, 1 Consign to Memory, 2 Force of Negation.

#### 25. Smallpox (B) — 1.0% of the meta
