# Correct recurrent certification review failures

## Scope

Repair the receiver-confirmed standard-review findings while preserving the structural outcome
firewall, deterministic event split, parent-only release, and candidate-profile authority cap.

## Acceptance criteria

- Distribution discrepancies smooth once over a shared union vocabulary; realistic materially
  different high-dimensional lists cannot pass the shipped margins.
- Confirmed curated/frozen semantic facts anywhere between historical and current endpoints veto
  certification; format-monitor observations never become confirmed authority by themselves.
- The family bootstrap resamples whole event clusters with replacement without deduplicating
  multiplicity, resamples relevant context, uses a shared family draw/reference, and keeps every
  prespecified candidate/channel in the simultaneous maximum even when guards abstain.
- Effective-event support is computed from observation/event weights (including context support)
  and concentrated multi-event samples abstain honestly.
- A checked-in outcome-blind control fixture is digest-bound to the profile; declared power and
  safely-inside parameters are either executed as designed and tested or removed from the authority
  contract. Promotion cannot expose placeholder diagnostics.
- Run identity/integrity hash-binds status and reasons; direct database tampering is refused.
- Facts after `certification_as_of` cannot perturb run identity/results, and both pending and
  unavailable format truth surface in run status.
- Focused adversarial controls, full era tests, Ruff, compileall, and immutable-store tamper tests
  pass. `uv.lock` remains excluded.
