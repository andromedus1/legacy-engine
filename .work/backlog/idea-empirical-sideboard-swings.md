The sideboard recommender's per-tag swing magnitudes are curated heuristic constants (_SWING_DEDICATED=0.20, _SWING_SOFT=0.10), explicitly NOT derived from before/after-sideboard win-rate data, and in thin windows most per-matchup in/out plans degrade to "rely on 15 composition." Mine game-level / pre- vs post-board results (where available) to make swing magnitudes and in/out plans empirical rather than estimated.
