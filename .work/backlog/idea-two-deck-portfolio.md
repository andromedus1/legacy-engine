Two-deck portfolio: pick a pair of decks that are foils of one another — both
high-agency, partitioning the field so each covers the other's bad matchups —
and choose per expected room. Andrew's anchor pairing: **Doomsday (his
Fantasticar tempo-juke build — owned in paper except the dual lands) + White
Beanstalk (Phelia/Riddler, decks/white-beanstalk-moxfield.txt)**. He likes his
Doomsday and has already identified it as high-agency; WB appears to be a great
pair. Open to other combinations, especially pairings anchored on his Doomsday.

Session evidence (2026-07-31, ranking-page ledger, corpus through 2026-07-30):

- Best-of-two share-weighted mean 55.5 vs ~53.3 for either deck alone;
  portfolio floor rises to ~48.7 on everything above 1.2% field share.
- The foil structure is cell-level real: WB covers DD's worst big cell (Izzet
  60.9 n=103 vs DD 43.2 n=114) plus the fair-blue cluster (Dimir Tempo 52.9,
  Azorius 57.1, Dimir Midrange 59.2, Cradle 51.9 vs DD 36.0); DD covers WB's
  soft cells (S&T 55.9 vs WB 44.1, Aluren 57.8 vs 45.6, Painter 48.9) plus
  taxes/combo/yard (D&T 70.3, TES 58.8, Grixis Rean 59.2, Energy 55.1).
- Internal cell near-even (WB vs DD 48.7 n=36) — they partition, not counter.
- Decision rule sketch: blue-heavy fair rooms → WB; taxes/prison/combo/
  graveyard rooms → DD.
- Shared hole (correlated weakness best-of-two can't fix): the colorless
  prison block — Eldrazi (WB 44.9, DD 43.3), Mystic Forge and Tron unmeasured
  by both. ~5% of current field.
- Practical: the two manabases don't collide (DD wants Underground Seas, WB
  wants Tundra/Trop/Savannah/Volcanic) so both stay sleeved; fetches and
  Forces are the contested cards; WB duals are likely binder gaps.
- Caveats logged: WB cells are ban-window cross-generation blends; Dimir Tempo
  n=9 / S&T n=8 thin; Andrew's Fantasticar Doomsday camp itself unmeasured
  (n=19 corpus decks); no reps on either 75.

Possible directions Andrew voiced or implied: validate the pairing thesis;
explore other high-agency pairings anchored on his Doomsday deck.
