Card-inclusion percentages are reported without foregrounding sample size: current-regime Dimir Tempo (n=11) showed "Flow State 100%", which over-stated a 7-week-old card on an adoption curve (5%->28%->100%) as a settled staple. Gate or asterisk inclusion % below an n threshold, show the adoption *trend* (not just a point estimate), and prefer a deeper window when the recent slice is thin.
