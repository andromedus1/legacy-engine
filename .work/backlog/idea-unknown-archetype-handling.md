'Unknown' is treated as a real opponent in fields and matchup rows (Doomsday vs Unknown n=88; 8.5% current-regime share) and in field-weighted math, adding noise by weighting win rates against an undefined bucket. Segregate Unknown (and Conflict(...) multi-labels) from field-weighted positioning math, or surface them separately, and track the Unknown fraction as a classifier-coverage metric to drive down.
