When designing decks and sideboards, the engine should emit a larger sideboard pool (~30 cards) rather than the standard 15 — capturing the "considering" cards a player actually weighs but doesn't make the final cut. In real play a pilot has their 60-card maindeck + 15-card sideboard + a pool of other cards they evaluated for those SB slots; having that extended toolset documented gives the player every option when piloting the deck and directly supports meta pivots and flex-slot decisions. Affects the deck-generation and sideboard-advisory design surfaces — output should include this "considering" tier alongside the committed 75.
