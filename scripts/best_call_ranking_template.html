      Method & sources

      Performance is expected match win rate against the full modeled field, including mirrors at 50%. Matchup floor is the lowest matchup estimate against any non-mirror opponent in that field. The high-floor pick maximizes floor independently; performance breaks ties. Blue tradeoffs are decks for which no shown eligible peer is better on both measures.

      Every valid matchup observation contributes. Compatible history supplies partial pooling; uncertain and unobserved matchups remain estimates. Ranges are 95% conditional posterior intervals. The range beside the floor belongs to the named toughest pairing. Uncertainty in the minimum across all opponents is available under Evidence details; it also allows for unobserved bad pairings. Neither a high estimate nor missing bad results guarantees no bad matchups.

      Floor coverage is the non-mirror field share meeting your minimum matchup n. Matchups below that threshold still use their observations and fitted priors; absent ledger cells use a weak 50% prior. Estimates condition on fitted historical priors and do not capture every pilot, event, or source-selection effect. Records and source windows are available in each dropdown.
